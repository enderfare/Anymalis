# Description
Lumithread Sericari are born in Radiance‑saturated valleys, solar ruins, and living light‑fields. Their silk glows with inner luminescence and stores magical patterns with exceptional clarity. They are truth‑seekers, illusion‑breakers, and beacons in the dark.

# Role & Culture
Their spires are built of crystal and silk, catching the sun. They are often judges, confessors, and spiritual guides. A Dawnwoven’s presence is said to make lies wilt.

# Lineage Trait – Revealing Silk
All Lumithread glows faintly. As an action, you may spend 1 ANI to flash your silk, revealing all hidden magical effects and invisible glyphs within 30 ft once per short rest. Additionally, when you weave a glyph with Lumithread, its visual signature is unmistakably radiant—it cannot be hidden by Veil borders.

# Choosing a Variant
A **Brilliance** weaver can create truth‑circles and sun lances; a **Glimmer** weaver can craft hope beacons and comfort cloaks. Your Intuitive Understanding shifts: Brilliance senses the truth of spoken words, Glimmer senses the safest path.
