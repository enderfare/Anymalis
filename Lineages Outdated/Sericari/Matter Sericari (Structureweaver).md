# Description
Structureweaver Sericari are born near mineral‑rich resonance deposits. Their silk can mimic the properties of stone, metal, glass, or even gaseous states. They are the ultimate material engineers, able to reshape the physical world thread by thread.

# Role & Culture
They build the great resonance‑forges and craft the most advanced Artificer tools. Their cities are studies in perfect material efficiency.

# Lineage Trait – Material Weaving
Once per short rest, you can spend 1 minute to alter the properties of an existing silk construct to another variant from your list. You gain proficiency with mason’s tools or smith’s tools. Your silk always feels cool and smooth to the touch, regardless of its current state.

# Choosing a Variant
Selecting **Transparency** makes you a master of invisible constructs; **Resonance** turns your silk into a sonic weapon. Your Intuitive Understanding gives you insight into the structural properties of all materials you touch.
