# Description
The Corveki are lore‑keepers and engineers. They trade in knowledge, contracts, and magical materials. Their cities hang impossibly from cliffs.

# Personality
Curious, cryptic, fond of riddles and layered agreements.

# Combat
They favor Artificer tools and aerial superiority, dropping resonance cartridges from above.

# Society
Libraries are their temples; contracts are sacred. Speech is often indirect, using metaphor.

# Magic
Many are Artificers with a focus on Connection borders, linking multiple glyphs into complex machines.
