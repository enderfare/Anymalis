## Status vocabulary

Use one of: `Not started`, `In progress`, `Blocked`, `Ready for review`, `Complete`. `Complete` means the content has been reviewed and validated, not merely copied.

## Per-file review gate

For each matrix row, verify:

- [ ] Source was inspected.
- [ ] Target ownership and disposition are clear.
- [ ] Content is migrated, merged, rewritten, or intentionally archived.
- [ ] Canon contradictions are resolved or logged.
- [ ] Formulas and terminology are consistent.
- [ ] Internal links and backlinks are checked.
- [ ] Matrix/CSV status is updated.
