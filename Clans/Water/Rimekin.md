# Rimekin

### Water Sub-Lineage

> **"Winter is not an ending. It is the world holding its breath."**

---

## Overview

The Rimekin are a people shaped by the **Frost Variant of Water Resonance**.

They are most commonly found in regions where Water Resonance and extreme cold overlap: frozen coasts, snowbound forests, high mountains, and places where winter refuses to leave.

To the Rimekin, frost is not simply frozen water.

It is a moment.

Water becomes still.

Sound becomes quiet.

The world slows.

And in that stillness, Resonance becomes easier to hear.

Rimekin culture embraces this contradiction. They are often lively, playful, and mischievous, yet they hold deep respect for silence and stillness.

A Rimekin may spend an afternoon playing tricks on their friends and the evening sitting alone beneath falling snow.

Neither behavior is considered contradictory.

Both are part of winter.

---

# Appearance

Rimekin share the physical characteristics of their parent Lineage, but prolonged exposure to Frost Resonance produces several distinctive traits.

### Physical Traits

* **Build:** Varies by parent Lineage.
* **Skin:** Often cool to the touch.
* **Eyes:** Frequently pale or unusually reflective.
* **Hair:** May naturally develop white, silver, pale blue, or translucent tones.
* **Distinctive Features:** Frost-like markings around the Resonant Scar are common.

When emotionally excited, their breath may become visibly misty even in warm environments.

---

# Frost Resonance

Rimekin do not create ice from nothing.

They manipulate the relationship between **Water and Temperature** through their Variant.

A skilled Rimekin can:

* Freeze existing water.
* Preserve objects in ice.
* Create frost along surfaces.
* Shape snow and ice.
* Draw moisture from the air.
* Produce cold mist.
* Reduce the temperature of a small area.

The strength of the effect depends on the character's Resonance and the environment.

# Culture

## Core Values

### Play

Winter can be dangerous.

Taking joy from it is therefore considered an act of defiance.

### Stillness

There is value in stopping.

A Rimekin is taught that not every problem needs to be solved immediately.

### Cleverness

Direct strength is useful.

A clever solution is better.

### Endurance

Winter teaches patience.

What survives the cold will eventually see spring.

---

# Society

Rimekin communities are usually small.

They tend to form around natural sources of Water Resonance such as frozen lakes, glaciers, snow forests, and mountain springs.

Their settlements often change throughout the year.

During warmer seasons, some communities migrate toward colder regions.

Others maintain underground or insulated settlements where their environment can remain stable.

---

# Traditions

## Frost Games

Children compete to create elaborate frost patterns.

The winner is not necessarily the person who creates the largest structure.

Creativity matters more than size.

Some communities turn this into a yearly festival.

---

## The First Frost

When a young Rimekin first demonstrates deliberate Frost Resonance, their family leaves a small symbol outside their home.

The symbol remains until the next natural thaw.

---

## The Silent Snow

Once each year, the community spends an entire night without speaking.

They gather beneath falling snow and simply listen.

The tradition is believed to strengthen their connection to Water Resonance.

---

# Mischief

Rimekin have a cultural fondness for harmless tricks.

Common examples include:

* Frosting someone's belongings overnight.
* Leaving tiny ice sculptures in unexpected places.
* Drawing patterns in frost on windows.
* Making footsteps appear to lead in the wrong direction.
* Creating snow figures that resemble sleeping travelers.

The line between harmless mischief and genuine cruelty is taken seriously.

A Rimekin who uses their abilities to humiliate or endanger someone is considered to have misunderstood the spirit of the tradition.

---

# Resonance

## Element

**Water**

## Variant

**Frost**

Frost is a specialized expression of Water Resonance associated with freezing, stillness, preservation, and cold.

---

## Resonant Affinity

Rimekin have a natural affinity for Frost Resonance.

This does not mean every Rimekin must become a powerful Resonance user.

Nor does it prevent them from developing other Water expressions.

---

# Lineage Traits

## Coldblooded

**Type:** Passive

Rimekin are naturally resistant to cold environments.

Extreme cold that would incapacitate another person is often merely uncomfortable to them.

---

## Frost Touch

**Type:** Resonance

A Rimekin can transfer a small amount of Frost Resonance through physical contact.

This can produce harmless frost or, with sufficient Resonance, freeze a small amount of exposed water.

---

## Winter's Step

**Type:** Utility

Rimekin can move across snow and thin ice with unusual ease.

Their footsteps are often lighter than expected, allowing them to cross snowy terrain without leaving obvious tracks.

---

## Frostcraft

**Type:** Utility / Resonance

A Rimekin can shape small amounts of ice and frost into simple temporary forms.

The creations are strongest when used for practical or artistic purposes.

Complex structures require significantly more skill and Resonance.

---

# The Rimekin and Other Water Users

Rimekin generally see themselves as one expression of Water rather than a completely separate tradition.

They often have friendly relationships with other Water Resonance users.

However, philosophical disagreements are common.

Some Water users see water as movement.

Rimekin see value in stillness.

Neither side considers the other wrong.

---

# Paths & Rimekin

The Frost Variant does not determine a character's Path.

| Path          | Rimekin Perspective                                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Scribe**    | Creates precise and beautiful Frost Glyphs                                   |
| **Channeler** | Directly channels cold through the body                                      |
| **Artificer** | Builds refrigeration, preservation, and winter devices                       |
| **Null**      | Considered particularly strange because of their relationship with Resonance |

### Scribes

Rimekin Scribes are known for delicate Frost Glyphs.

Their constructions often resemble snowflakes, crystalline structures, or intricate geometric patterns.

### Channelers

Rimekin Channelers tend to be the most visibly supernatural.

Their Resonance can manifest as freezing breath, frost-covered limbs, or sudden drops in temperature.

### Artificers

Rimekin Artificers have developed techniques for preserving food, materials, medicines, and other perishables.

### Nulls

Rimekin communities have an unusual relationship with Nulls.

The absence of Resonance is especially noticeable against a culture where Resonance is deeply integrated into everyday life.

# Life

## Childhood

Rimekin children are encouraged to explore.

Their first lessons are often practical rather than academic:

* How to recognize unsafe ice.
* How to survive a snowstorm.
* How to find water.
* How to keep warm.
* How to distinguish harmless Frost Resonance from dangerous cold.

Mischief is tolerated.

Cruelty is not.

---

## Adulthood

Adulthood is often marked by surviving a night alone in the winter wilderness.

The challenge is not necessarily combat.

It is proving that the individual understands the environment.

---

# Settlements

Rimekin settlements often incorporate ice directly into their architecture.

Ice can be used for:

* Temporary walls
* Storage
* Decorations
* Bridges
* Tools
* Resonance structures

The best settlements are designed to change with the seasons.

---

# Relations

### Other Water Communities

Generally friendly.

Rimekin see themselves as part of the broader Water tradition.

### Fire Resonance Users

Usually treated with good-natured suspicion.

The classic Rimekin joke is:

> "We can work this out. Just don't stand too close."

### Nulls

Respected, but difficult for many Rimekin to understand.
