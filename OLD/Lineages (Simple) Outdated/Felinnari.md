# Description
The Felinnari are precision‑obsessed wardens. Their society values discipline, territorial honor, and the control of resonance. They are often seen as aloof but make unmatched guardians.

# Personality
Stoic, proud, ritualistic. They distrust chaos and impulsive magic.

# Combat
Felinnari excel at hit‑and‑run tactics, using natural agility and resonance‑warding to shut down enemy mages.

# Society
Built into cliffs or giant trees, their settlements are fortified sanctuaries. They maintain strict coming‑of‑age trials.

# Magic
Many become Channelers or Scribes specializing in Guardian and Containment borders. Their birthmarks often resemble protective wards.
