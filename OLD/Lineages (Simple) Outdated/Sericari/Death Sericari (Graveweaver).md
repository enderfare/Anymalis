# Description
Graveweaver Sericari are the most solemn of the Silkborn. Their silk preserves resonance echoes of the deceased, and they serve as morticians, ancestral record‑keepers, and executioners of the undead. To the Funeral Woven, death is not an enemy but the final border that gives life its shape.

# Role & Culture
They dwell in crypt‑monasteries, mausoleum‑cities, and necropolis sanctuaries. Their ceremonies are elaborate, their silks often woven directly into burial shrouds. Other lineages treat them with reverence; to insult a Graveweaver is to insult the dead.

# Lineage Trait – Merciful Cocoon
All Graveweaver Sericari can weave a **funeral cocoon** around a recently deceased creature (within 1 hour). While wrapped, the corpse cannot be raised as undead, and its resonance echoes linger clearly, allowing you to cast *Speak with Dead* without material components once per day. The cocoon is made of silk that matches your variant’s colour (soft grey for Passing, frayed brown for Decay, or eternally frayed black for End).

# Choosing a Variant
Your variant defines your relationship with death: a Passing weaver brings peace, a Decay weaver brings entropy, an End weaver brings finality. Each grants unique passive and active abilities (resistance to possession, rusting touch, or spell‑killing, respectively) and unlocks specialized silk constructs like the **Final Knot** or **Stasis Shroud**.
