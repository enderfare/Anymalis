# Description
Umbrasilk Sericari hatch in eclipsed forests, void caverns, and dead resonance zones. Their silk absorbs light, sound, and magical traces, making them natural spies, saboteurs, and keepers of forbidden secrets. To be Veilwoven is to understand that the most powerful magic is the magic no one sees.

# Role & Culture
Umbrasilk settlements are hidden—built inside hollowed mountains, beneath canopy layers that never see the sun, or within permanent resonance shadows. They trade in information, whispered contracts, and the subtle removal of problems. Other peoples view them with a mixture of respect and unease; a Veilwoven’s smile is never quite readable.

# Lineage Trait – Light Consumption
Regardless of variant, all Umbrasilk Sericari gain:
- **Shadow Affinity:** You have advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness.
- **Silk Property:** Your silk can reduce magical visibility; as an action you can create a 20‑ft sphere of dim light centered on a silk object that also suppresses magical light sources within. Once per long rest.
- **Woven Glyph Bonus:** Glyphs woven with Umbrasilk gain the Veil border effect for free (the glyph is harder to detect).

# Choosing a Variant
At character creation, select either Void or Umbra. This choice determines the exact appearance of your silk, your Passive/Active affinity effects, and your Intuitive Understanding. It also unlocks the unique Silk Integration ability described in the variant’s file (e.g., a Void Sericari can create a silencing blindfold, an Umbra Sericari can weave a cloak of near‑invisibility).
