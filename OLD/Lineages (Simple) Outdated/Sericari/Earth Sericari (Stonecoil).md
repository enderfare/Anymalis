# Description
Stonecoil Sericari are born in deep resonance caverns, mineral‑rich ranges, and crystal cathedrals. Their silk is as tough as the bones of the world. They are builders, miners, and guardians of the underworld’s secrets.

# Role & Culture
Their citadels are carved from living rock, supported by woven silk pillars stronger than steel. They value endurance, craftsmanship, and the slow patience of geology. A Stonecoil’s word is a contract written in stone.

# Lineage Trait – Mineral Cocoon
Your silk constructs gain **+2 AC** and **+10 HP**. Additionally, once per long rest you may crystallise a section of silk into a permanent, non‑magical gem worth 1d10×10 gp. The silk’s appearance and secondary properties (magnetism, fertility, luminescence) depend entirely on the variant you choose.

# Choosing a Variant
A Stonecoil who selects **Crystal** will weave shimmering, light‑refracting threads; one who chooses **Mountain** will produce heavy, grey silk that anchors reality. Your variant grants unique passive senses (e.g., detecting hidden metal for Metal, sensing cave networks for Cave) and unlocks constructs like **Gem of Power** or **Avalanche** glyphs.
