# Description
Thermoweaver Sericari live near thermal boundaries—volcanic vents, frozen geysers, and steam valleys. Their silk regulates temperature, making them master survivalists, climbers, and negotiators of elemental extremes.

# Role & Culture
They are often found in extreme‑environment outposts, acting as mediators between fire and ice. Their looms incorporate heating and cooling elements, and their clothing is a microclimate.

# Lineage Trait – Thermal Silk
You are immune to environmental temperature penalties. As an action, you can change your silk’s variant among Heat, Cold, or Equilibrium, altering the temperature of anything it wraps. Your silk never degrades from heat or frost.

# Choosing a Variant
A **Conduction** Thermoweaver can drain heat from a foe’s armour to heal themselves; an **Insulation** weaver can craft an invincible survival blanket. Your Intuitive Understanding reveals heat signatures, thermal leaks, or melting points.
