# Description
Verdant Sericari are born in immense resonance‑jungles where life grows unchecked. Their silk behaves like living plant matter—growing, healing, and even reproducing. They are the healers, the midwives, the gardners of flesh and ecosystem.

# Role & Culture
Their Loom‑Temples are living structures, woven from silk and tree alike. They value symbiosis and patience; a Verdant Sericari believes every thread is part of a greater web of life.

# Lineage Trait – Living Silk
Your silk is inherently alive. It can slowly regenerate when damaged (regains 1 HP per minute), and you may animate a silk rope to extend up to 50 ft and bind to surfaces as an action. Once per long rest, you can weave a cocoon around a willing creature that grants resistance to bludgeoning damage for 1 hour.

# Choosing a Variant
A **Growth** Verdant shapes expanding vines that become temporary bridges; a **Connection** Verdant strings threads between allies that redirect healing. Your Intuitive Understanding also varies—from sensing plant health to feeling the emotions of all nearby creatures.
