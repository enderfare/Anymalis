# Pulse Outline

Pulse makes a spell cyclical — it beats like a heart, activates in waves, or pulses energy over time.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Rhythmic Activation:** The spell's effect triggers at fixed intervals (every round, every 10 seconds) for the duration.
- **Modulation:** You can choose to pulse at half strength but double frequency, or vice versa.
- **Synergy:** When linked with Connection, all pulsed spells synchronise their rhythm.

## Example Spell
**Heartbeat of the Earth** — Foundation (Earth‑Stone) + Pulse + Containment. Every 10 seconds, the ground trembles in a 30‑ft radius, knocking creatures prone.
