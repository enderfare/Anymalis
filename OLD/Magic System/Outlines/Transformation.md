# Transformation Outline

Transformation alters the nature of the spell or its target over time. It can turn fire into ice, stone into mud, or a human into a beast.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Metamorphosis:** The spell's element can shift to another that is not its opposite (requires an additional Foundation outline).
- **Polymorph:** The target's form changes according to a template you define.
- **Gradual Shift:** The change occurs over the spell's duration rather than instantly.

## Example Spell
**Stone to Sand** — Foundation (Earth‑Stone) + Transformation (Earth‑Sand) + Duration. A 10‑ft cube of solid rock becomes loose sand over 1 minute.
