# Trigger Outline

Trigger defines the condition under which the glyph activates. It turns spells into traps, alarms, or contingent blessings.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Set Condition:** Choose one: proximity (enters 10 ft), sound (specific word), touch, time delay (up to 1 hour), or magical signature (when a specific spell is cast nearby).
- **Reaction:** The glyph can activate as a reaction to its condition even if you're not present.
- **Layered:** You may add multiple Trigger outlines for complex conditions (proximity AND word).

## Example Spell
**Alarm Glyph** — Foundation (Wind‑Whisper) + Trigger (proximity) + Echo. Sends a silent alert to the caster when triggered, repeating every round the intruder remains.
