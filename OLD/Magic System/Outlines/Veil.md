# Veil Outline

Veil hides the glyph's appearance, magical aura, and activation signature. It is the spy's best friend.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Invisible Glyph:** The glyph cannot be seen by normal vision; *detect magic* reveals it only if the caster succeeds on a contested check.
- **Silent Activation:** The spell makes no sound when triggered.
- **False Signature:** You can make the glyph appear as a different element to inspection.

## Example Spell
**Hidden Trap** — Foundation (Fire‑Spark) + Veil + Trigger (touch). An invisible rune that shocks the first creature to touch it, leaving no trace of its origin.
