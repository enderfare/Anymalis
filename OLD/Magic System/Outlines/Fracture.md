# Fracture Outline

Fracture intentionally destabilises resonance to break, shatter, or dispel existing magic. It is the outline of last resort — and the Null's mimicry.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Destabilise:** Target a spell, glyph, or enchanted item; it makes a saving throw or ends.
- **Overload:** If used on your own spell, the spell detonates for double damage in double area, then ends.
- **RST Drain:** Using Fracture costs 1 RST temporarily.

## Example Spell
**Glyph‑Shatter** — Foundation (Darkness‑Void) + Fracture + Amplification. A pulse that ends all spells of 3rd level or lower within 30 ft.
