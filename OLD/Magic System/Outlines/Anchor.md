# Anchor Outline

Anchor locks reality around the glyph, preventing disruption, dispelling, or temporal erosion. It is mandatory for Time magic and permanent wards.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Anti‑Dispel:** The glyph has advantage against dispelling attempts.
- **Permanence:** When combined with Duration ×2, the spell becomes permanent until broken.
- **Temporal Lock:** The spell is immune to time‑altering effects (slow, haste, time stop).

## Example Spell
**Eternal Ward** — Foundation (Earth‑Mountain) + Anchor + Duration ×2. A stone barrier that stands for centuries, self‑repairing from minor damage.
