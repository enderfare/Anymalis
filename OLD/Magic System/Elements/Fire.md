# Description
Fire is raw, consuming energy. It is the element of passion, destruction, and renewal. Fire resonance is aggressive, hungry, and difficult to contain—fuel feeds it, but control defines the mage. Those born with Fire affinity often have a restless spirit; they are warriors, forgers, and destroyers.

# Manifestation
When channeled, Fire manifests as real flame, heat shimmer, or a deep inner warmth. The color ranges from soft orange (Hearth) to violent white‑blue (Blaze). Sound accompanies: a crackling, a roar, or a gentle hum. The smell of burning—incense, smoke, or hot metal—often lingers.

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Blacksmiths, combat mages, light‑bringers, executioners, healers (Hearth variant).
- **Myths/Legends:** The First Flame is said to be a gift from a dying star; the Wildfire variant is considered cursed, often linked to mages who lose control and become living conflagrations.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Glyph Examples:**  
  - *Flame Lash* (Flame, Amplification, Precision) – a whip of fire.  
  - *Cinder Shroud* (Cinder, Veil) – choking ash that conceals.  
  - *Hearthskin* (Hearth, Duration, Pulse) – warmth and stamina regeneration.  
- **Special Rules:** Fire glyphs drawn with Ember Ash ink gain +2 damage but risk backlash on critical failure. Wildfire glyphs must include a Containment border or they spread uncontrollably.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A swirling, smoke‑like pattern that pulses orange, red, or gold. In Hearth variants the mark resembles a stylized flame cupped in gentle hands; in Wildfire it looks like a chaotic explosion frozen on the skin.
