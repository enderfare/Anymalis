# The Mythic Meta‑Glyphs

While most scholars classify magic through the Eleven Elements, ancient forbidden texts speak of two forces that exist beyond the elemental hierarchy entirely. These are not considered true “elements.” They are known as **Meta‑Glyphs** — fundamental principles underlying all resonance, glyphs, borders, and reality itself.

Unlike ordinary elemental glyphs, Meta‑Glyphs do not govern aspects of existence. They govern the **possibility** of existence.

---

## The Two Singular Meta‑Glyphs

For detailed individual profiles, including affinity effects, resonance manifestations, and sample bearers, see:
- **[[Prima]]** — The First Spark (Meta‑Glyph of Possibility)
- **[[Nihil]]** — The Final Silence (Meta‑Glyph of Cancellation)

Below is a summary of their cosmic roles.

---

### Prima — The First Spark
**Tier:** Mythic
**Variants:** 1
**Classification:** Meta‑Glyph of Possibility

Prima is believed to be the origin of all resonance. It does not merely create matter or life. Creation governs: possibility, definition, emergence, conceptual existence, and the formation of new resonance structures. According to ancient theory, every glyph in existence is merely a fragmented echo of Prima’s original pattern. Where ordinary glyphs manipulate existing laws, Prima can theoretically **create entirely new relationships between concepts**.

#### Mythological Role
Creation myths describe Prima differently: the first sound, the first circle, the first thought, the first distinction between “something” and “nothing.” Some traditions claim the universe itself is a partially stabilised Creation glyph still unfolding.

#### The Principle of Possibility
Prima does not “summon.” It allows reality to become capable of things that were previously impossible. A Prima‑linked phenomenon may generate unknown materials, invent spontaneous resonance pathways, alter glyph interactions, create new biological mutations, produce impossible colours or sounds, or cause unfinished concepts to manifest physically.

---

### Nihil — The Final Silence
**Tier:** Mythic
**Variants:** 1
**Classification:** Meta‑Glyph of Cancellation

Nihil represents the principle opposing possibility itself. It does not destroy; it **cancels**. Annihilation removes definition, connection, resonance, and conceptual continuity. Where Death ends life, Nihil ends existence within reality’s structure. Objects affected by Nihil are not ruined or erased traditionally — reality behaves as though they were never properly defined to begin with.

#### Mythological Role
Ancient apocalyptic texts refer to Nihil as The Unwriting, The Last Silence, The Hole Between Stars. Some traditions claim Nihil was necessary to prevent reality from expanding infinitely under Prima’s influence. Without termination, creation would never stabilise.

#### The Principle of Cancellation
Nihil denies the resonance connections that allow something to exist meaningfully. This may result in forgotten names, erased magical bonds, vanished sounds, broken causality, or unstable memory continuity.

## Resonance Overlap Theory

No mortal can truly “possess” a Meta‑Glyph. Instead, individuals may experience **Resonance Overlap** — a condition where a person’s resonance partially synchronises with a Meta‑Glyph principle. This explains why mythical bearers survive at all, display inconsistent abilities, and cannot fully control their phenomena.

Overlap is not affinity; it is a **condition of cosmic proximity**. See the individual Meta‑Glyph files for the mechanical effects of such overlap.

---

## Resonance Stability (RST)

To explain Mythic phenomena, scholars introduced **Resonance Stability (RST)**.

**Formula:** `Insight + Endurance + Training`

When resonance output exceeds RST, reality destabilises around the user.

### Overflow Stages
| Stage | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Minor** | Glowing air, spontaneous activations, emotional resonance leaks |
| **Moderate** | Living glyph formation, unstable spell behaviour, environmental distortion |
| **Severe** | Conceptual inconsistencies, memory fragmentation, impossible geometry |
| **Catastrophic** | Localised unreality; physical laws become temporarily unstable |

### The Cancellation Principle
When Prima and Nihil resonances overlap, distortions stabilise, resonance quiets, impossible effects cease, and overflow collapses harmlessly. This state is called **Equilibrium**. Some scholars believe the current world exists because the two principles once partially neutralised one another, creating stable reality from absolute extremes.

## Cultural Factions
- **Primists:** Worship Prima as the divine source of existence. Believe creation and innovation are sacred acts.
- **Nullists:** View Nihil as purification from imperfection and suffering. Often extremist.
- **Harmonists:** Believe both Meta‑Glyphs must remain balanced and dormant. Most major magical academies follow Harmonist teachings officially.

---

## Historical Accounts
- **The Stillborn Prophet:** A child born with shifting marks that changed shape continuously. Flowers reportedly bloomed from stone nearby. Died within hours of birth.
- **The Empty King:** An ancient ruler whose portraits and records cannot remain consistent across copies. Believed by some to have been Nihil‑linked.
- **The Veil War:** A forbidden conflict where factions attempted to artificially create Meta‑Glyph bearers through resonance experimentation. Most records are incomplete or contradictory.

> *“No human was ever meant to fully wield either force.”* — Harmonist maxim
