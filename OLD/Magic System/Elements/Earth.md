# Description
Earth is the foundation. It shapes, endures, and resists. Its nine variants span from fertile soil to unyielding mountain, making Earth mages the ultimate builders, miners, and protectors. Though less explosive than fire or wind, Earth’s power is the power of time itself—slow, patient, unstoppable.

# Manifestation
Casting Earth causes the ground to tremble faintly, the scent of wet stone to rise, and the air to feel heavier. Glyphs often appear chiselled or inlaid, with a solid, three‑dimensional weight.

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Architects, miners, city‑wall engineers, resurrection smiths.
- **Myths/Legends:** The Eternal Forge is said to be a pocket realm where Earth and Fire mages hammer raw resonance into artefacts.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Earth glyphs drawn on stone gain +2 stability. Metal variant can interface with Artificer devices.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A crackled, layered symbol like a cliff face, sometimes with tiny embedded crystals that catch the light.
