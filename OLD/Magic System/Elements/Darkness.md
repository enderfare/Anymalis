# Description
Darkness is not evil—it is the necessary quiet, the void that defines form. It removes: light, sound, memory, presence. Those aligned with Darkness are spies, assassins, protectors of secrets, and keepers of the forgotten.

# Manifestation
Casting Darkness causes light to dim, sounds to muffle, and the air to grow cool. The caster’s outline may blur, and the glyph itself seems to drink in ambient resonance.

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Night guards, intelligence agents, morticians, monks of silence.
- **Myths/Legends:** The Void is said to be a glimpse into the space between stars; many believe that excessive use erases the caster’s own identity.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Darkness glyphs automatically suppress visible magical auras. Void glyphs can consume other active spells.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A shadow‑like stain that shifts slowly, or a precisely outlined black pattern like spilled ink.
