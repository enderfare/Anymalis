# Description
Umbra is the art of shadows—subtle, indirect, and ever‑present. It is the variant of spies and guardians.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can meld into any shadow large enough to cover you.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you may become invisible for 1 round as a reaction to being targeted.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You sense the exact position of every shadow within 60 ft.
