# Description
Cinder is the aftermath. It is the ghost of fire, the final whisper of consumed matter. Cinder mages create choking clouds, blinding ash‑storms, and landscapes of desolation. They are often employed as cleaners, mortuary assistants, or saboteurs.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can exhale a cloud of fine ash in a 10‑ft cone that lightly obscures vision and causes enemies to cough (disadvantage on Stealth) for 1 round.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can touch a corpse or a 5‑ft pile of organic material and reduce it to sterilised ash in 1 minute. This prevents the target from being raised as undead.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense any hidden fire by the cinders it leaves behind, even days later.
