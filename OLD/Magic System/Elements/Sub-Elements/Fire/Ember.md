# Description
Ember is fire’s patience. It burns low and long, holding heat for days. It is the variant of survivalists, night‑watchmen, and those who guard the flame through the dark.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can touch an object and have it retain warmth for 1 hour per level.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can scatter a handful of embers that stick to enemies, dealing 1d4 fire damage per round for 1 minute.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense hidden caches of coal or peat underground.
