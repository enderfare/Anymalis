# Description
Hearth is the gentlest face of fire. It represents home, sanctuary, and the fire that warms but does not consume. Hearth mages are healers of the spirit, guardians of sanctuaries, and keepers of hope. In many cultures, they are considered blessed — a living promise that fire need not always destroy.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Allies within 10 ft of you gain advantage on saving throws against fear and charm effects.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a **Hearthfire** at a point within 30 ft. The fire lasts for 8 hours, cannot be extinguished by wind or rain, and grants all friendly creatures who rest nearby an additional 1d6 temporary HP that lasts until their next long rest.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can tell if a person is lying by the “warmth” of their words (not mind reading, but a gut feeling of sincerity).
