# Description
Spark is the catalyst. It is the first flicker, the lightning in the flint. Spark mages are not pyromaniacs; they are technicians. They start engines, detonate traps, and deliver precisely controlled jolts. Their magic often blurs the line between Fire and Wind (lightning).

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may remotely ignite any flammable object you can see within 30 ft as a bonus action.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can touch a metal surface and send a jolt of electricity through it, stunning anyone in contact for 1 round.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can read the “spark of life” — you know if a creature is alive, dying, or dead just by looking at them.
