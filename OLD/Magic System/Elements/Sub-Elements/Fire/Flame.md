# Description
Flame is the archetypical Fire variant — reliable, bright, and hungry. It is the most common manifestation, used by smiths, soldiers, and hearth‑keepers alike.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can light or extinguish a candle‑sized flame with a thought.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can project a 15‑ft cone of fire dealing 2d6 damage (Dex save half).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact moment a fire will go out.
