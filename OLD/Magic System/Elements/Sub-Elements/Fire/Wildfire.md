# Description
Wildfire is destruction unchained. It is the living conflagration, the rage of nature that consumes everything. Users are feared — and often consumed by their own power.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your fire spells ignore resistance to fire, but you take 1 point of unresistable fire damage each time you cast one.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can ignite a 30‑ft radius area into a roaring wildfire that lasts 1 minute, dealing 3d6 fire damage per round to all inside.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can predict exactly where a fire will spread next.
