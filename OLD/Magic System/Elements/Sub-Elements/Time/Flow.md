# Description
Flow is the relentless march of seconds. It quickens the user, hastens events, and allows impossible bursts of speed.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You gain an extra reaction each round.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can take an additional action on your turn.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact time and can count seconds perfectly.
