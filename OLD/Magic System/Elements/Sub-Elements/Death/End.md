# Description
End is the final stop. It unexists processes—not just life, but spells, memories, even elemental effects. It is the closest mortal magic comes to Nihil.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Spells you cast that kill a creature cannot be reversed by any means short of a Prima‑linked miracle.
- **Active:** Once per day, you can attempt to end a magical effect permanently (DC 15 + spell level).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You sense the exact moment of any creature’s death within a mile.
