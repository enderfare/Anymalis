# Description
Glimmer is the gentle aspect of Light—it leads, comforts, and reassures. It is often seen as the patron variant of wanderers and lost souls.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Allies within 10 ft have advantage against fear effects.
- **Active:** Once per rest, you can create a shimmering path visible only to allies that lasts 1 hour.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the emotional state of anyone you touch.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Hope Beacon | Flow, Resonance | Allies can see a distant light that guides them to you. |
| Comforting Glow | Duration, Pulse | A warm light that removes exhaustion for one hour. |

# Cultural Role
Glimmer users often become healers of the spirit, traveling priests, or diplomatic emissaries.
