# Description
Stone is the most straightforward Earth aspect—durable, immovable, and protective.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can meld into natural stone for up to 1 minute, once per long rest.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can raise a 5‑ft high, 10‑ft wide stone wall (AC 17, 50 HP) as an action.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can detect hollow spaces and hidden passages behind stone.
