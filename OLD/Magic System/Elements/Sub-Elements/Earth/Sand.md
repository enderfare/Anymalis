# Description
Sand is the abrasive, flowing side of Earth. It blinds, smothers, and erodes even the hardest stone.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your hands exude a thin layer of sand, allowing you to grip things firmly (advantage against being disarmed).
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can whip up a sandstorm in a 15‑ft cone that blinds enemies for 1 round.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can tell the exact time by observing the fall of sand grains.
