# Description
Mountain is the monumental, slow, and unstoppable aspect of Earth. It embodies resilience and cataclysmic power.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage and can shatter stone.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can cause a localized tremor (30 ft radius) that knocks creatures prone and collapses fragile structures.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can predict landslides and cave‑ins.
