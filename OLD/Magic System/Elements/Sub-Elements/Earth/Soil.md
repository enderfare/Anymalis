# Description
Soil is the nurturing aspect of Earth. It feeds nations and buries secrets.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may cause seeds to sprout in your hand.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can create a 10‑ft patch of thick mud that counts as difficult terrain and may entangle creatures.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can find the best burial site to prevent undead rising.
