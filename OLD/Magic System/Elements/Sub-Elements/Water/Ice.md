# Description
Ice is water’s solid form—unyielding, sharp, and preserving. It traps, shatters, and endures.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your touch deals 1 point of cold damage; you may freeze a 1‑gallon container of water over 1 minute.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can create a wall of ice (10 ft × 10 ft × 1 ft, AC 13, 30 HP) that lasts 1 hour.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can feel the exact thickness of ice on any body of water.
