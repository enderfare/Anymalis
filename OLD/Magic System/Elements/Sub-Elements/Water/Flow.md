# Description
Flow is the mastery of moving water. Rivers, streams, and waves obey your call. It is the most common Water variant.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may walk on water as long as you end your turn on a solid surface or use a reaction to stay afloat.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can create a strong current in a body of water that pulls creatures 30 ft in a direction of your choice.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can purify dirty water by touch, making it drinkable.
