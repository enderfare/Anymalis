# Description
Deep is the crushing weight of the ocean’s heart. It is pressure, darkness, and the ancient silence below.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You have darkvision 60 ft underwater, and your melee attacks deal +1d4 bludgeoning damage while submerged.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a sphere of crushing pressure (20 ft radius) for 1 minute, dealing 3d6 bludgeoning damage per round to creatures inside.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the structural weakness of any object that has been underwater.
