# Description
Spring is the pure, life‑giving aspect of water. It cleanses poison, heals wounds, and renews hope.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can create up to 1 gallon of fresh water per day from thin air.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a temporary hot spring that heals 2d8 HP for any creature that bathes in it for 1 hour.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can diagnose illnesses by tasting a person’s sweat or tears.
