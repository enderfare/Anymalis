# Description
Mist is the veil between worlds—water’s most insubstantial form. It hides, confuses, and softens the world.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your movement leaves no trace in damp environments.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can turn invisible for 1 round while inside mist or fog.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense the presence of invisible creatures in fog; they leave ripples.
