# Description
Still water is the mirror of the soul. It represents calm, reflection, and hidden depths.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can perfectly still any water you touch, making it as smooth as glass.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a reflective barrier of water that acts as a shield (+2 AC, deflects one ranged attack).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know if a body of water is poisoned or tainted by touch.
