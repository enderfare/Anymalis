# Description
Phase‑Change governs the transitions between states. Melt ice, boil water, condense steam—this variant is the key to fluid control.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can freeze water in a 5‑ft cube per round or thaw it equally fast by touch.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you may instantly vaporize a 10‑ft cube of water, creating a cloud of steam that heavily obscures vision and deals 2d6 fire damage to anyone inside.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can tell the purity of any liquid by its freezing/boiling point.

| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |

# Cultural Role
Found among alchemists, distillers, and any culture that relies on desalination or ice harvesting.
