# Description
Conduction is the flow of heat along paths. It can drain warmth from an enemy’s armor or pre‑heat a forge from across the room.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may take heat from a fire and channel it into a weapon (giving it +1 fire damage for 1 minute) by spending an action.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can touch a creature and pull heat from its body, dealing 2d6 cold damage and healing yourself for the same amount.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the thermal conductivity of any material and can find the warmest spot to start a fire.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Heat Siphon | Flow, Precision | Drain heat from a target, slowing them and dealing cold damage. |
| Thermal Bridge | Connection, Duration | Link two objects so they share temperature instantly. |

# Cultural Role
Used by smiths to control forge temperatures and by assassins to freeze victims without leaving marks.
