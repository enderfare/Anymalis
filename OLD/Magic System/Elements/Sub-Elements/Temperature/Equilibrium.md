# Description
Equilibrium is the master of thermal neutrality. It brings things back to a safe, stable state and is the foundation of climate control.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can stabilize a dying creature’s body temperature, granting advantage on their death saving throws.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can negate all fire and cold damage in a 30‑ft radius for 1 round.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact thermal threshold any material can withstand before breaking.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Balance Field | Containment, Duration | A room stays at 21°C regardless of outside weather. |
| Thermal Dampen | Resonance, Pulse | Reduces fire/cold damage by 2d6 for allies in range. |

# Cultural Role
Equilibrium users are peacekeepers, climate‑engineers, and often act as mediators between fire and ice factions.
