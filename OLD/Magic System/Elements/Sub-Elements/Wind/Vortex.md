# Description
Vortex is the cyclone, the dust devil, the tornado. It spins, gathers, and tears apart.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your melee attacks have a 10% chance to create a 5‑ft swirling wind that imposes disadvantage on ranged attacks against you for 1 round.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can summon a 15‑ft radius whirlwind that lasts 1 minute, picking up small objects and creatures and dealing 2d8 slashing damage per round.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can feel the Coriolis force and always know true north.
