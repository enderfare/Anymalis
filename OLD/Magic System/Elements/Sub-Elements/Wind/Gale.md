# Description
Gale is the fierce, driving wind. It propels, knocks down, and clears the path.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can extinguish a torch by pointing at it.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can release a burst of wind that pushes all creatures within 10 ft back 15 ft and deals 1d6 damage.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense the presence of any creature within 60 ft that is airborne or leaping.
