# Description
Current is the invisible river in the sky. It lifts, guides, and connects distant places.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can glide for 1 minute per point of Endurance, descending 1 ft for every 5 ft traveled.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can grant yourself a fly speed of 60 ft for 10 minutes.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the best route to sail or fly to any destination within 100 miles.
