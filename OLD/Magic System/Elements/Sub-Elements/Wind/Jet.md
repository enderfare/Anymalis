# Description
Jet is the concentrated, high‑pressure blast of wind. It propels rockets, punches holes, and turns air into a cutting blade.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can blast a 15‑ft cone of compressed air with your palm, extinguishing flames and pushing small objects (5 lbs) as a bonus action once per round.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can perform a jet‑assisted charge: fly up to 60 ft in a straight line and make a melee attack with +2d6 damage on hit.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense the exact PSI of any gas container.
