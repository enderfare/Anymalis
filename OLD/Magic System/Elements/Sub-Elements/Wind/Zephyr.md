# Description
Zephyr is the swift, silent, almost invisible wind. It is the element of scouts and messengers.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from enemies you have moved past this turn.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can turn invisible for 1 round as a bonus action while moving.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact position of any moving creature within 30 ft that is not standing perfectly still.
