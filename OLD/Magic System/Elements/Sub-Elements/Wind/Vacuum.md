# Description
Vacuum is the absence of air. It suffocates, silences, and crushes by pressure differential.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can hold your breath for 10 times the normal duration.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can create a 10‑ft radius sphere of vacuum for 1 round, extinguishing flames, silencing sound, and forcing creatures to hold their breath or begin suffocating.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact location of any air leak in a structure.
