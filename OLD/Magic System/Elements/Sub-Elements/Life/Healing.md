# Description
Healing mends the body. It is the most widely accepted Life variant and the core of battlefield medicine.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** When you stabilize a dying creature, they regain 1 HP.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can remove one condition (poison, disease, paralysis) with a touch.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You always know the exact location of the most injured person within 100 ft.
