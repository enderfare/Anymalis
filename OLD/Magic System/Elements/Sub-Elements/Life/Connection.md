# Description
Connection is the web of life. It links minds, shares strength, and unites allies.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may use a reaction to take half the damage an adjacent ally receives.
- **Active:** Once per rest, you can link two allies so they share healing for 1 minute.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know if a creature is lying by its biological reactions (sweat, heart rate).
