# Description
Life is the surge of existence. It heals, creates, binds, and multiplies. Users are healers, gardeners of flesh, and protectors of ecosystems.

# Manifestation
Casting Life brings the scent of fresh rain, the sound of growing things, and a vibrant green glow. The glyph may sprout small plants temporarily.

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Doctors, midwives, druids, community leaders.
- **Myths/Legends:** A Birth mage once created an entire sentient species from a single seed.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Life glyphs are uniquely receptive to organic surfaces (skin, wood) and gain a +2 stability bonus when used on living targets.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A vibrant, organic mark that may change seasonally, sprouting tiny buds or dropping leaves.
