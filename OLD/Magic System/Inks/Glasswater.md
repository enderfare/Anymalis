# Description
Glasswater is ink that catches the light. It shimmers like a soap bubble and dries into a thin, transparent film. It is the ink of illusionists, memory‑weavers, and anyone who wants their spell to bounce, echo, or multiply.

# Effects on Glyphs
- **Echo Amplification:** When using an Echo outline, the spell repeats an additional time (3 times total instead of 2).
- **Refraction:** You may spend 1 extra ANI to have the spell split into two identical rays that target different creatures.
- **Fragility:** If you take damage while casting, the glyph has a 25% chance to shatter and deal 1d4 force damage in a 5‑ft burst.

# Common Uses
- Mirror‑based scrying and illusion duplication.
- *Glass Rain Archive* and other memory‑recording spells.
- Water (Still variant) reflection magic.

# Resonance Silk Interaction
When used with Tidewoven or Lumithread silk, Glasswater creates **Prismatic Thread** that can store a single image or reflection and replay it when touched. This silk is used in Corveki message‑tapestries.
