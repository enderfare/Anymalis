# Description
Verdant Sap is alive. It pulses faintly and smells of crushed leaves and wet earth. Spells cast with it are not so much drawn as they are **planted** — they take root and grow into their effect. Healers, druids, and Verdant Sericari treasure it.

# Effects on Glyphs
- **Duration:** Spells last twice as long when using a Duration outline.
- **Regeneration:** Any healing spell regains an additional 1d4 HP per ANI spent.
- **Surface Bond:** The ink bonds with living wood or skin, making the glyph semi‑permanent and able to be reactivated once per day without redrawing.

# Common Uses
- *Hearthskin*, *Mend Flesh*, and regeneration cocoons.
- Long‑term enchantments on living wood structures.
- Birth spells that need to sustain a new organism.

# Resonance Silk Interaction
Verdant Sericari silk dyed with this sap becomes **Living Silk**: it can slowly self‑repair, and any healing cocoon woven with it restores an additional 1d6 HP. The silk itself may sprout tiny leaves if left in sunlight.
