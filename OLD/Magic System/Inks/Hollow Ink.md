# Description
Hollow Ink is an absence given liquid form. It looks like a smear of darkness and makes no sound when applied. Mages who use it are often spies, assassins, or Null‑touched Scribes who wish to hide their work. The ink consumes light and resonance; a glyph drawn with it is nearly undetectable until it activates.

# Effects on Glyphs
- **Veil Outline Free:** Any glyph drawn with Hollow Ink is considered to have a Veil outline applied for free (the glyph is invisible until triggered).
- **Silence Aura:** While the ink is wet, it suppresses sound in a 5‑ft radius.
- **Surface Decay:** Non‑magical surfaces erode after 1 use; paper and wood crumble to dust after a single casting.

# Common Uses
- Traps, hidden wards, and delayed‑activation runes.
- Assassination glyphs that leave no trace.
- Void and Umbra spells (Darkness affinity).

# Resonance Silk Interaction
Umbrasilk Sericari who dye their threads with Hollow Ink produce **Silent Weaving** silk: glyphs made with it have no visible resonance signature while inactive, and the silk itself grants advantage on Stealth checks.
