# Description
Silverthread is the scribe's finest tool. It flows like liquid mercury and dries into a faintly glowing silver calligraphy. It is the ink of choice for archivists, temporal mages, and anyone who needs a glyph to function exactly as intended, without surprises.

# Effects on Glyphs
- **Stability:** Glyphs drawn with Silverthread gain a +2 bonus to Stability checks.
- **Precision:** When you use a Precision outline, the outline's effect is doubled (e.g., area halved becomes quartered, range doubled).
- **Elemental Affinity:** Time and Light glyphs cast with this ink cost 1 less ANI.

# Common Uses
- *Still* and *Flow* Time spells that require absolute accuracy.
- Truth‑circles and revelation wards (Light/Brilliance).
- Permanent archival glyphs on stone or metal.

# Resonance Silk Interaction
Silverthread blends naturally with Sericari silk. A Sericari who uses this ink as a dye for their silk can embed a Precision outline permanently into the thread, making it ideal for microscopic glyph weaving.
