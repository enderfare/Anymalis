# Rumors

Rumors represent information circulating about a character.

A rumor may be:

```text
True
Partially True
False
Unknown
```

Examples:

```text
"They survived a Hollow Storm."

"They stole the Crown's Resonance Engine."

"They cannot be affected by Resonance."

"They defeated a Master Scribe."
```

## Rumor Lifecycle

```text
Event
↓
Witness
↓
Story
↓
Rumor
↓
Widespread Belief
↓
Reputation Tag
```

Rumors do not need to be true to affect NPC behavior.

The GM should track who believes a rumor and how widely it has spread.

A false rumor can therefore produce real social consequences.
