# Reputation Tags

Reputation Tags are concise statements that summarize what people associate with a character.

Examples:

```text
"The Glyphbreaker"
"Hero of the Hollow"
"Master Artificer"
"Royal Agent"
"Thief"
"Monster"
"Resonance Scholar"
"Walking Disaster"
"Unreliable"
"Heretic"
```

## Mechanical Use

A relevant Tag may provide:

```text
+2
```

to an appropriate social check.

A Tag can also create a penalty when its meaning damages credibility.

## Social Permission

Tags may provide narrative permissions even when no numerical bonus applies.

Example:

```text
"Royal Agent"
```

may allow access to government facilities where an ordinary character would need an invitation.

Tags should create consequences, not merely bonuses.
