# Path Abilities

Path abilities are narrative/mechanical abilities tied to a character's Path.

They do not replace the existing Path mechanics.

## Path Grades

```text
Initiate
Practitioner
Adept
Master
```

These are progression grades, not character levels.

## Scribe

### Initiate — Glyph Preparation
Improve preparation of existing Glyph structures.

### Practitioner — Rapid Inscription
Reduce preparation time for appropriate Glyph work.

### Adept — Glyph Weaving
Maintain increasingly complex prepared structures.

### Master — Perfect Script
Once per scene, preserve an appropriate Glyph structure that would otherwise fail from a non-magical disruption.

## Channeler

### Initiate — Body Resonance
Access basic Channeler techniques.

### Practitioner — Forced Flow
Improve efficiency of controlled Resonance through the body.

### Adept — Overchannel
Temporarily exceed ordinary safe Strain at meaningful physical cost.

### Master — Living Conduit
Maintain extreme Resonance output longer before physical collapse.

## Artificer

### Initiate — Field Repair
Repair damaged Resonance equipment.

### Practitioner — Modular Construction
Modify appropriate devices during field operations.

### Adept — Overclock Device
Push a device beyond its normal safe operating range.

### Master — Living Workshop
Construct complex Resonance machinery under field conditions.

## Null

### Initiate — Dead Sense
Detect suppressed, broken, or absent Resonance.

### Practitioner — Glyph Break
Interrupt Glyph structures through trained Null techniques.

### Adept — Dead Zone
Project a localized anti-Resonance field.

### Master — Absolute Silence
Temporarily suppress a larger Resonance interaction.

Exact effects should be reconciled with the existing Path rules before publication.
