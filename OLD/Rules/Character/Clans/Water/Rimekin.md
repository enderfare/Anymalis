# Rimekin

*The Frostbound Mischief-Makers*

Rimekin are a people shaped by **Frost Resonance**, a Variant of the Water Element.

They are creatures of snow, ice, frozen water, and winter.

Yet despite their association with harsh environments, Rimekin culture is rarely grim.

They are playful, curious, mischievous, and difficult to pin down.

They do not believe winter should merely be endured.

**They believe it should be played in.**

## Frostborn

Rimekin bodies are naturally adapted to freezing environments.

Their skin is often cool to the touch, and frost may form naturally around them when they become excited, frightened, or angry.

**Mechanical Effect:** Rimekin ignore normal penalties caused by extreme cold and gain resistance to environmental Frost effects.

## Frost Touch

Rimekin can draw Water Resonance from moisture around them and rapidly reduce its temperature.

With sufficient moisture, they can create thin layers of frost or ice.

**Mechanical Effect:** A Rimekin can create or manipulate a small amount of frost through direct contact. The effect is normally insufficient to cause major structural changes unless reinforced with Resonance.

## Winter's Step

Rimekin move naturally across snow and ice.

Their feet and bodies instinctively adjust to slippery surfaces.

**Mechanical Effect:** Rimekin do not suffer normal movement penalties from snow or ice and gain an advantage when attempting to remain balanced on slippery terrain.

## Frost Affinity

Rimekin possess a natural affinity for:

**Water → Frost**

Their affinity provides:

* Reduced Resonance consumption when using Frost.
* Easier stabilization of Frost effects.
* Greater precision when freezing moisture.
* Improved resistance against Frost Resonance backlash.
* Instinctive understanding of Frost Resonance.

## Frost Resonance

Frost is not a separate Element.

It is a **Variant of Water**.

Rimekin therefore understand Frost as water in a different state and expression.

They can manipulate moisture, freezing, ice formation, and cold through their connection to Water Resonance.

## Frostcraft

Rimekin commonly create small Frost Resonance effects as part of everyday life.

Examples include:

* Frost lanterns.
* Ice tools.
* Frozen locks.
* Temporary bridges.
* Snow shelters.
* Decorative frost patterns.
* Ice sculptures.
* Chilled food storage.

## Mischief

Mischief is an important part of Rimekin culture.

Pranks are often used to test creativity, awareness, and social bonds.

A traditional prank might involve:

* Frosting someone's doorway.
* Leaving tiny ice footprints.
* Freezing a cup to a table.
* Covering a sleeping person's belongings in harmless frost.
* Creating an elaborate snow creature outside someone's home.

Cruelty, however, is considered fundamentally different from mischief.

A prank that causes genuine harm is generally considered a failure of judgment.

## Paths

Rimekin can follow any of the Four Paths.

Common choices include:

* **Scribes** — frost-glyph artists and Resonance tricksters.
* **Channelers** — agile hunters and skirmishers.
* **Artificers** — creators of ice tools and Frost devices.
* **Nulls** — rare individuals whose relationship with Resonance is fundamentally different.

## Character Hooks

A Rimekin character might:

* Have left their frozen homeland for the first time.
* Be searching for an ancient Frost Resonance site.
* Be famous for elaborate harmless pranks.
* Have accidentally frozen something they were never supposed to touch.
* Serve as a guide through a dangerous frozen region.
* Believe they have discovered a new expression of Frost Resonance.
* Be fascinated by warm climates and completely unprepared for them.
