# Illuminated

*Those Who See the Resonance*

The Illuminated are people whose bodies and senses have been profoundly altered by Light Resonance.

They are not necessarily born into a single bloodline.

Illumination may occur through ancestry, prolonged exposure, Resonance accidents, rituals, or other unknown circumstances.

## Resonant Sight

Illuminated individuals can perceive aspects of Resonance that ordinary senses cannot easily detect.

They may notice:

* Faint Resonance trails.
* Residual glyph patterns.
* Disturbances in magical fields.
* Hidden Light Resonance.
* Recently used glyphwork.
* Certain Resonant Scars.

**Mechanical Effect:** Illuminated characters gain an advantage when attempting to detect or identify visible Resonance phenomena.

## Luminal Awareness

Light Resonance makes subtle changes in the environment easier for an Illuminated character to notice.

**Mechanical Effect:** Illuminated characters gain proficiency in a perception, investigation, or insight-based skill related to detecting hidden information.

## Radiant Presence

Illuminated individuals often possess an unmistakable presence.

Their eyes may shine.

Their Resonant Scars may glow.

Their skin may reflect light unnaturally.

This can make hiding difficult, but it can also make them difficult to ignore.

**Mechanical Effect:** The character gains a minor bonus to social checks involving revelation, authority, honesty, or gaining attention, at GM discretion.

## Resonance Affinity

Illuminated individuals possess a natural connection to the **Light Element**.

Their specific Variant varies.

Two Illuminated characters may therefore express completely different forms of Light Resonance.

## Light Affinity

The Light Affinity provides:

* Reduced Resonance consumption when using Light.
* Easier stabilization of Light Glyphs.
* Greater precision with Light-based effects.
* Improved resistance against Light Resonance backlash.
* Instinctive understanding of Light Resonance.

## Paths

Illuminated individuals can follow any of the Four Paths.

Common paths include:

* **Scribes** — researchers, investigators, archivists, and glyph scholars.
* **Channelers** — Resonance hunters and protectors.
* **Artificers** — creators of Light-based instruments.
* **Nulls** — rare individuals whose existence creates an unusual contradiction with their Light-touched nature.

## Common Roles

* Investigator
* Scholar
* Archivist
* Glyph Inspector
* Researcher
* Explorer
* Watcher
* Temple Keeper
* Resonance Analyst

## Character Hooks

An Illuminated character might:

* See Resonance traces nobody else can perceive.
* Have been changed by a mysterious Light Resonance event.
* Search for the source of their Resonant Scar.
* Be studying an ancient Light Glyph.
* Have witnessed something hidden beneath an illusion.
* Be unable to determine whether their visions are memories or Resonance echoes.

> **"Light reveals. It does not explain."**
