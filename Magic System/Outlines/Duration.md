# Duration Outline

Duration extends a spell's lifespan, maintaining its effect for minutes, hours, or even days. It is essential for wards, buffs, and preservation magic.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Extend:** The spell's duration increases by one step: round → minute, minute → 10 minutes, 10 minutes → 1 hour, 1 hour → 8 hours.
- **Concentration:** If the spell requires concentration, you may add a second Duration outline to make it self‑sustaining for 10 minutes after you stop concentrating.
- **Stasis:** When combined with Anchor, the spell becomes permanent until dispelled.

## Common Interactions
- Duration + Trigger = a spell that lies dormant and then lasts for hours.
- Duration + Transformation = a slow, gradual polymorph.
- Duration + Containment = a barrier that lasts a full day.

## Example Spell
**Stasis Cocoon** — Foundation (Time‑Still) + Duration + Duration + Containment. Freezes a willing creature in a protective cocoon for up to 8 hours.
