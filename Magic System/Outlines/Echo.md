# Echo Outline

Echo causes a spell to repeat its effect after a short delay, like a stone skipping across time.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Repetition:** The spell activates again at the start of your next turn (or after 1 round) with the same parameters.
- **Stackable:** Multiple Echo outlines add additional repetitions (Echo ×2 = 3 activations total).
- **Echo Cost:** Each echo costs 1 ANI, paid at the initial casting.

## Example Spell
**Echoing Strike** — Foundation (Time‑Cycle) + Echo + Amplification. A weapon attack that hits twice, the second strike manifesting a moment later.
