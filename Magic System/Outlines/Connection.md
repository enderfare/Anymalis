# Connection Outline

Connection links multiple glyphs, creatures, or objects, allowing shared effects, telepathic networks, or chained spells.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Link:** Up to your INT modifier creatures/objects are magically linked for the spell's duration.
- **Shared Effect:** A spell cast on one linked target affects all others at half potency (if harmful) or full (if beneficial).
- **Network:** You can communicate telepathically with all linked beings.

## Example Spell
**Unity Shield** — Foundation (Life‑Connection) + Connection + Duration. Distributes damage taken by any linked ally equally among all.
