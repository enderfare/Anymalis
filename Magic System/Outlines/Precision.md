# Precision Outline

Precision narrows the spell's effect to an exact point, creature, or body part. It is the sniper's outline, used for assassination, surgery, and delicate glyph work.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Single Target:** The spell affects only one creature or a 5‑ft cube.
- **Accuracy Bonus:** +2 to the spell attack roll, or the target's saving throw DC increases by 2.
- **Critical Chance:** On a natural 18‑20, the spell deals double damage or the effect is maximised.

## Common Interactions
- Precision + Echo = a second strike on the same target after a delay.
- Precision + Veil = invisible sniper shot.
- Precision + Containment = the spell can target a specific organ (called shot, requires a separate check).

## Example Spell
**Heartseeker** — Foundation (Wind‑Jet) + Precision + Amplification. A needle‑thin gust that pierces armour, dealing 2d8 force and ignoring cover.
