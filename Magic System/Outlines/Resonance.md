# Resonance Outline

The Resonance outline (distinct from the Matter element variant) synchronises the glyph with ambient resonance or other active spells, amplifying or harmonising effects.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Resonance Feedback:** The spell's effect increases by +1 per active spell of the same element within 60 ft.
- **Harmonise:** All active spells within range have their duration refreshed by 1 round.
- **Cancellation:** If overlapping with an opposing element, both spells make a contested check; loser fizzles.

## Example Spell
**Choir of Flames** — Foundation (Fire‑Hearth) + Resonance + Connection. Links campfires; each fire within range increases the warmth and healing of all others.
