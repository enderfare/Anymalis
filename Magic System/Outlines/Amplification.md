# Amplification Outline

Amplification increases the raw output of a spell — damage, healing, range, or effect intensity. It is the most dangerous outline, as it strains the glyph's containment.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Damage/Healing:** Add one extra die of the spell's base type (d6 becomes 2d6).
- **Range/Area:** Double one dimension (range, radius, number of targets).
- **Instability:** Each Amplification outline beyond the first causes a cumulative 10% chance of backlash.

## Common Interactions
- Amplification + Containment = safe overcharging (backlash reduced).
- Amplification + Fracture = intentional explosion; area becomes hazardous terrain.
- Amplification + Duration = spell intensity increases each round it's active.

## Example Spell
**Overcharged Ember** — Foundation (Fire‑Ember) + Amplification + Amplification. A single ember that detonates into a 20‑ft fireball, dealing 4d6 fire damage (25% backlash chance).
