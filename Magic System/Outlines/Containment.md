# Containment Outline

Containment keeps magic where it belongs — inside a defined space. It prevents unwanted spread, backlash, and resonance leakage. Crucial for Wildfire and Void magic.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Boundaries:** The spell's effect cannot exceed a defined area (sphere, cube, or line).
- **Backlash Reduction:** Reduces the chance of instability by 20%.
- **Imprisonment:** Can be used offensively to trap a creature inside a spell effect.

## Example Spell
**Wildfire Cage** — Foundation (Fire‑Wildfire) + Containment + Amplification. A raging fire contained in a 10‑ft sphere; safe outside, inferno inside.
