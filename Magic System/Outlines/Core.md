# Outlines

A glyph without outlines is raw, directionless resonance. Outlines define **how** the spell behaves — its duration, target, power, and interaction with other magic. Simple spells use one or two outlines; master‑level glyphs layer a dozen.

## The Fifteen Outlines

| Outline | Function | Common Pairings |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| [[Foundation]] | Defines the elemental identity of the glyph. | Required in every spell |
| [[Flow]] | Controls directional movement of the effect. | Movement, teleport, wind |
| [[Amplification]] | Increases intensity, damage, or output. | Damage spells, overcharging |
| [[Precision]] | Narrows the target area or increases accuracy. | Sniping, single‑target debuffs |
| [[Duration]] | Extends the spell's lifespan. | Buffs, wards, stasis |
| [[Trigger]] | Sets activation conditions (proximity, time, sound). | Traps, alarms, delayed spells |
| [[Containment]] | Prevents overflow, keeps magic within bounds. | Wildfire, explosive spells |
| [[Transformation]] | Alters the spell's nature over time. | Polymorph, transmutation |
| [[Connection]] | Links multiple glyphs or creatures. | Network spells, shared HP |
| [[Resonance (Outline)]] | Synchronises the glyph with nearby spells. | Amplifying others, harmonic casting |
| [[Echo]] | Repeats the spell effect after a delay. | Recursion, after‑images |
| [[Anchor]] | Stabilises reality around the spell; prevents disruption. | Time spells, permanent wards |
| [[Veil]] | Conceals the glyph's appearance and magical aura. | Traps, secret messages |
| [[Pulse]] | Creates rhythmic, repeated activation. | Heartbeat spells, regeneration |
| [[Fracture]] | Intentionally destabilises resonance for destructive effects. | Anti‑magic, shattering enemy glyphs |

Outlines are drawn as concentric rings or interlocking shapes around the core glyph. The order matters — a Flow outline outside an Amplification outline means “amplify then direct”; reversing it directs then amplifies, potentially hitting different targets.
