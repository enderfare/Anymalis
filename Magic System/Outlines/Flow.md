# Flow Outline

Flow controls the directional movement of the spell's resonance. It can shape a beam, a cone, a wall, or a guided missile. It is the difference between an explosion and a lance.

## Mechanical Effect
- **Define Shape:** Choose one: line (60 ft), cone (15 ft), sphere (10 ft radius), or guided (follow target for 1 round).
- **Movement Bonus:** Spells that move you (teleport, haste) gain +10 ft range or +5 ft speed.
- **Channel:** If used with Pulse, you can redirect the effect each round as a bonus action.

## Common Interactions
- Flow + Amplification = a massive, narrow beam (double damage but must hit).
- Flow + Connection = chain lightning effects.
- Flow + Veil = invisible attack; undetectable until impact.

## Example Spell
**Wind Lance** — Foundation (Wind‑Gale) + Flow + Amplification. A focused gust that deals 3d6 bludgeoning and pushes 20 ft.
