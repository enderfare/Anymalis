# Description
Matter is the bedrock of the physical world. It defines mass, texture, transparency, and state. Masters are the ultimate engineers, able to reshape materials.

# Manifestation
Matter glyphs often appear solid and three‑dimensional, even when drawn on flat surfaces. They may emit a low hum or cause objects to vibrate.

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Architects, craftsmen, miners, armorers.
- **Myths/Legends:** The Matter Forge, a legendary workshop where raw resonance is hammered into solid metal.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Matter glyphs can permanently alter non‑magical materials if a high enough ANI is invested.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A blocky, almost runic mark that feels like stone or metal to the touch.
