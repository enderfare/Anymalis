# Description
Light is the element of truth, purity, and presence. It exposes, illuminates, and sometimes blinds. Users of Light are often seers, judges, or healers of the mind. Its power is concentrated—fewer variants mean each is overwhelmingly potent.

# Manifestation
Casting Light brings a soft, warm radiance or a searing beam. It hums with a frequency that resonates in the bones, and the air around a Light glyph grows still, as if reality itself is paying attention.

# Variants
| Variant | Description |

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Oracles, inquisitors, lighthouse keepers, illusion‑breakers.
- **Myths/Legends:** The first Brilliance mage was said to have seen the face of a god and gone blind from the beauty.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Glyph Examples:**  
  - *Revealing Ray* (Brilliance, Precision) – dispels illusions and invisibility.  
  - *Guiding Star* (Glimmer, Flow, Duration) – a floating mote that leads the way.  
- **Special Rules:** Light glyphs automatically reveal hidden resonance, nullifying Veil borders within range.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
Intricate, sweeping arcs like sunlight through a prism, often shimmering gold or silver. Brilliance marks are stark, almost eye‑shaped; Glimmer marks are scattered like stardust.
