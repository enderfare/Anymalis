# Description
Death is the natural closure. Not cruelty, but inevitability. It ends life, motion, sound, memory, even magic itself. Its users are guides, executioners, and guardians of the cycle.

# Manifestation
Casting Death drains color from the air, leaves a chill, and creates a sense of profound stillness. The glyph itself often appears as if drawn with ash.

# Variants
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| [[Passing]] | Gentle release, easing souls to rest. |
| [[Decay]] | Entropy, corrosion, gradual ruin. |
| [[End]] | Absolute, instant cessation. |

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Morticians, executioners, mercy‑killers, undead hunters.
- **Myths/Legends:** The End variant is said to be a fragment of Nihil itself.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Death glyphs cannot heal; any attempt to mix them with Life causes a catastrophic resonance failure. End glyphs require a Will save from the caster to avoid personal soul damage.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A cold, gray symbol that sometimes seems to weep shadows.
