# Description
Time is the most unstable element. It governs progression, suspension, and repetition. Mages of Time are rare, often obsessed, and their magic strains the very fabric of reality.

# Manifestation
Casting Time causes the air to shimmer, sounds to echo or stretch, and objects to briefly flicker between states. The caster may appear to move in unnatural bursts or stand eerily still.

# Variants
| Variant | Description |

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Oracles, strategists, historians, executioners of the eternally condemned.
- **Myths/Legends:** A Time mage who attempts to alter the past inevitably erases themselves from existence.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Time glyphs always require an Anchor border or they risk temporal feedback. Casting Time in rapid succession causes SAN damage.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A glowing, slow‑turning spiral that can appear as an hourglass, clock face, or ouroboros.
