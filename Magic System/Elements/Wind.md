# Description
Wind is the breath of the world—swift, elusive, and ever‑changing. Its ten variants offer unmatched speed and versatility, from gentle breezes to screaming vacuums. Wind mages are messengers, scouts, and saboteurs, their magic quick to cast but sometimes lacking raw force.

# Manifestation
Casting Wind stirs the air, brings the scent of distant places, and plays with sound. Glyphs often appear in motion, drawn with a single flowing stroke.

# Variants
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| [[Breeze]] | Gentle air, cooling, message‑carrying. |
| [[Gale]] | Strong gusts, propulsion, knockback. |
| [[Zephyr]] | Swift, silent, near‑invisible movement. |
| [[Whisper]] | Sound transmission, ventriloquism, secrecy. |
| [[Vacuum]] | Absence of air, silence, suffocation. |

| [[Jet]] | Compressed, high‑pressure blasts, cutting force. |

# Cultural Significance
- **Common Roles:** Couriers, sailors, aerial scouts, weather‑mancers.
- **Myths/Legends:** The Stormwind variant is said to be a gift from the sky spirits; its users can ride lightning.

# Mechanical Notes
- **Special Rules:** Wind glyphs are always quick to activate (‑1 action time). Jet and Vacuum require high ANI but offer great control.

# Sample Birthmark Appearance
A series of flowing, overlapping swooshes like wind‑over‑sand, often on the limbs, that quiver subtly.
