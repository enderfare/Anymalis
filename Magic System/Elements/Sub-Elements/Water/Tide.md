# Description
Tide is the rhythmic heart of the ocean—the push and pull governed by celestial dance. Tide mages can raise sea levels, create maelstroms, or drag enemies back and forth across the battlefield. They are revered by sailors and feared by coastal settlements.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can cause the water level in a 30‑ft radius to rise or fall by 5 ft over the course of 1 minute by concentrating.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can create a 20‑ft‑wide wave that travels 60 ft, extinguishing fires and sweeping away creatures (Strength save or be pushed 15 ft).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can communicate basic concepts with any aquatic creature.
