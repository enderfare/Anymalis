# Description
Rain brings life and gloom. It nourishes crops, obscures vision, and cleanses the land. Rain mages are often seen as omens.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may cause light rain in a 20‑ft radius centered on you at will (extinguishes small flames, calms dust).
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can summon a downpour in a 100‑ft radius that heavily obscures sight and extinguishes all non‑magical fires for 10 minutes.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can tell if rain is natural or magically induced.
