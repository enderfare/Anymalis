# Description
Metal is Earth refined—strong, conductive, and deadly. It is the foundation of industry and warfare.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may repair a broken metal weapon or armor piece by holding it for 1 minute.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can magnetize a metal object, causing it to stick to surfaces or attract/repel other metal.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can detect hidden metal objects within 30 ft (like a metal detector).
