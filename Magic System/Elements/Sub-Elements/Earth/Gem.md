# Description
Gem is the treasure of Earth—beauty, power, and focus. It amplifies magic and stores spells.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can store up to 3 ANI worth of resonance in a gem each day, to be released when you cast a glyph.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you may crush a gem worth at least 100 gp to empower a single spell, maximizing its damage or doubling its area.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense the presence of precious stones within 60 ft.
