# Description
Crystal is the structured beauty of Earth. It focuses light, stores energy, and resonates perfectly.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may store one glyph (up to 3 ANI) in a crystal you hold, releasing it later as a bonus action.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a crystal prism that splits a single‑target spell into two identical rays.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact value and properties of any gem.
