# Description
Cave is the hidden, protective earth—the womb of the mountain. It shelters, echoes, and conceals.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your voice can carry a whisper perfectly to any point within a cave or enclosed space.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can merge with a cave wall and travel through connected stone at half speed for 1 minute.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You always know the quickest exit from any cave system.
