# Description
Stormwind is the tempest—wind and lightning born together. It is the fury of the sky made manifest.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your melee attacks deal an extra 1d4 lightning damage while you are outdoors and the sky is visible.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can call down a lightning bolt from the sky (or from a storm you create) dealing 8d6 lightning damage in a 5‑ft wide, 120‑ft line.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can start a fire by focusing static electricity between your fingers.
