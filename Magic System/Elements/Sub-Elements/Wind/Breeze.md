# Description
Breeze is Wind’s gentlest touch—a comfort, a messenger, and a subtle guide.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can lighten your fall, taking no damage from falling 20 ft or less.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can send a message on the wind to a specific person within 1 mile.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact wind speed and direction at all times.
