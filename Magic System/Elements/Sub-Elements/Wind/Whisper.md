# Description
Whisper is the voice of the wind. It carries secrets, spreads rumors, and delivers silent commands.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can mimic any voice you have heard for 1 minute once per short rest.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can create a zone of silence that moves with you for 10 minutes.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can identify a person by the sound of their breathing.
