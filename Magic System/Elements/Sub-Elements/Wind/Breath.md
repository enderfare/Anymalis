# Description
Breath is the vital wind—the air of life. It fills lungs, steadies hearts, and can be stolen from enemies.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may share your breath with an ally by touching them, allowing them to breathe without air for 1 hour.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can exhale a 30‑ft cone of clean air that removes poison gases and wakes sleeping allies.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You feel the respiration of every living creature within 60 ft.
