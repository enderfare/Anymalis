# Description
Heat is the accelerating motion of particles. It warms, forges, and can drive reactions. Bearers often have a fiery temperament without ever touching true fire.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your touch can warm objects to 40°C. You can dry wet materials instantly.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you may cause a metal object within 30 ft to become red‑hot for 1 minute, dealing 1d4 fire damage on contact.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact temperature of any object you touch and can tell if it is magically heated.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Heat Metal | Amplification, Duration | Metal object glows red‑hot, damaging those in contact. |
| Warmth Aura | Containment, Pulse | Radiates comfortable heat in a 20‑ft radius for 1 hour. |

# Cultural Role
Heat users are valued in cold climates, kitchens, and forges—they are the invisible flame behind many crafts.
