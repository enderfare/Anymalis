# Description
Cold is stillness at the molecular level. It preserves, slows, and deadens. Users are often calm, detached, and invaluable in desert survival.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your touch can chill objects down to freezing. You never suffer heatstroke.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you may freeze a 5‑ft cube of water or quench a small fire instantly.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact temperature of anything you touch and can detect living things by their heat signature.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Frost Sheath | Containment, Duration | A thin layer of ice coats a weapon, adding 1d4 cold damage. |
| Cool Sanctuary | Flow, Amplification | Creates a zone of pleasant coolness even in a desert. |

# Cultural Role
Cold mages are prized in forges (quenching), food preservation, and by any desert‑dwelling culture.
