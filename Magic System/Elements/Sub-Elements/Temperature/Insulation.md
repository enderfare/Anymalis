# Description
Insulation is the barrier against temperature. It preserves cold storage, protects against flames, and keeps armor from cooking its wearer.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can touch hot coals or liquid nitrogen with no harm.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can make an ally’s armor perfectly insulated for 1 hour, granting resistance to fire and cold damage.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the R‑value of any material and can build a perfect survival shelter in minutes.

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Heat Ward | Containment, Duration | Prevents heat from entering or leaving a 10‑ft cube. |
| Frostward Cloak | Veil, Duration | Keeps the wearer warm and dry in the harshest blizzard. |

# Cultural Role
Essential in arctic expeditions, volcano treks, and food preservation. Often paired with Cold mages.
