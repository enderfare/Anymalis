# Description
Birth is pure creation. It can spawn temporary life, new organs, or even simple organisms. Feared when misused.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can cause seeds to germinate instantly in your palm.
- **Active:** Once per week, you may perform a ritual to create a permanent, loyal Tiny creature (stats as per GM).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the genetic lineage of any creature you touch.
