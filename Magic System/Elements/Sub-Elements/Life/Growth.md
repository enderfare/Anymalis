# Description
Growth is unchecked expansion—useful for agriculture, combat buffs, and creating barriers.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can increase your own size by one category for 1 minute, once per short rest.
- **Active:** By touching a plant, you can shape it into simple forms (bridge, wall).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can identify every plant species you see.
