# Description
Prima is the Meta‑Glyph of Possibility. It is the original spark from which all resonance is believed to have descended. It does not command existing reality; it **introduces new possibilities** into the weave of existence. A person with Resonance Overlap to Prima is not a caster of Creation — they are a living conduit for the universe’s capacity to innovate.

The mark of Prima never stays the same; it shifts, dreaming of forms that have not yet been defined. Those who bear it are often overwhelmed by the sheer pressure of what could be.

# Resonance Overlap Affinity Effects
These effects manifest in individuals who have partial synchronisation with Prima. They cannot be turned off; they happen involuntarily unless the bearer actively suppresses them (requiring a DC 20 Presence check and causing 1d6 SAN damage).

## Passive Manifestations
- **Ambient Innovation:** Magical items within 30 ft occasionally gain temporary new properties (e.g., a sword glows faintly, a wand produces harmless sparks). The GM decides when and how.
- **Living Glyphs:** Any glyph you draw has a 5% chance to animate, becoming semi‑alive. It may attempt to improve itself, which can be beneficial or catastrophic.
- **Conceptual Leak:** Your dreams sometimes become visible to nearby sleepers; they might see fragments of impossible places or languages that do not exist.

## Active Manifestations
Once per long rest, you may deliberately channel Prima to produce one of the following effects. Each use costs 2d4 SAN damage.

1. **Spontaneous Creation:** You create a non‑magical object no larger than 1 cubic foot. The object is a physical approximation of a concept you hold in mind. It lasts for 1 hour.
2. **Possibility Surge:** For 1 minute, any spell cast within 60 ft has a 20% chance to gain an additional, randomly generated beneficial effect (GM discretion).
3. **Unfinished Glyph Awakening:** You touch a broken or incomplete glyph and it activates as if it were finished, with unpredictable but generally positive results.
4. **Innovate Self:** You temporarily gain a mutation (roll on the Resonance Scar table) that lasts for 10 minutes.

## Risk of Overlap Overload
If you use Active Manifestations more than once per day, or if you are exposed to intense creation magic, you must make a RST check. Failure leads to **Prima Overload**:
- Your body begins to glow with all colours at once.
- You uncontrollably create small objects (flowers, crystals, strange trinkets) that spill from your hands.
- You speak in a language that did not exist until you spoke it.
- After 1d4 rounds, the overload ends, but you permanently gain 1 ATS (as your body mutates toward something new) and lose 2 SAN.

# Sample Phenomena of Prima Bearers
- A child who painted a door on a wall, and the door became real for one night.
- A smith who accidentally forged a sword that could cut lies.
- A musician whose notes caused the audience to see the colour of sound.

# Resonance Silk Integration (Hypothetical)
No Sericari lineage is naturally aligned with Prima. However, a Luminaris of Prima affinity may weave silk that appears as threads of pure, shifting light. Such silk is impossibly unstable and cannot be used for reliable glyphs, but if a Sericari attempted to cultivate it, they would risk immediate Luminal Overload.

# The Great Balance
Harmonists teach that Prima’s influence must be balanced by Nihil. Without the Final Silence to give form a limit, creation would expand into infinite, maddening complexity.
