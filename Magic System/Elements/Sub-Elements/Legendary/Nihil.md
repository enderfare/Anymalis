# Description
Nihil is the Meta‑Glyph of Cancellation. It is not destruction but the **removal of definition**. It is the principle that allows things to end, limits to exist, and reality to have edges. A person overlapped with Nihil is a walking termination point — magic falters near them, memories blur, and the very concept of their existence feels provisional.

The mark of Nihil is often an un‑mark: a shape defined by what it is not. To look at it is to feel a quiet, existential dread, as if the mark is looking back with the void’s patience.

# Resonance Overlap Affinity Effects
These effects occur naturally around a Nihil‑linked individual and can be consciously directed with terrible cost.

## Passive Manifestations
- **Dead Zone Aura:** Creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 12 Will save to cast spells or maintain concentration. If they fail, the spell fizzles. This cannot be turned off; you can only suppress it for 1 round by taking 1 SAN damage.
- **Memory Erosion:** People who spend more than a day in your presence begin to forget minor details about you — your voice, your eye colour, the sound of your footsteps. If they part company for a week, they remember you only as a vague unease.
- **Item Failure:** Magical items in prolonged contact with you (e.g., carried) have a cumulative 5% chance per day to become mundane for 1 hour.

## Active Manifestations
Once per long rest, you may invoke Nihil to produce one of the following. Each use costs 2d4 SAN damage and permanently removes a minor, non‑vital memory from your own mind (like a childhood song or a face from years ago).

1. **Cancel Spell:** You touch a magical effect and attempt to end it permanently (as *dispel magic* cast at 9th level, but works on any spell; DC 15 + spell level, using your Presence).
2. **Unwrite Name:** You speak a creature’s name (if you know its true name) and attempt to erase it from reality’s registry. The creature must make a DC 20 Charisma save or be erased from all written records, memories, and scrying attempts for 24 hours. It still physically exists but is unrecognisable by name.
3. **Silence the World:** For 1 minute, you project a sphere of absolute, dead silence (100 ft radius). No sound can exist within. You cannot move or take any other action while maintaining this.
4. **Final Word:** You touch a creature that is dying or at 0 HP. It cannot be healed or stabilised by any magic short of Prima’s direct intervention for 24 hours.

## Risk of Overlap Overload
Using more than one active ability per day, or being hit by a powerful creation spell, forces an RST check. Failure triggers **Nihil Overload**:
- You become invisible and intangible for 1d6 rounds; you can observe but not interact.
- All ongoing spells within 100 ft end.
- Everyone within 100 ft must make a Wisdom save or forget the last hour.
- After the overload, you permanently lose 1d4 Charisma (PRS) as your identity erodes, but gain +1 to all saves against magic.

# Sample Phenomena of Nihil Bearers
- An infant whose crying silenced a thunderstorm; the village forgot his name by morning.
- A scholar who discovered a book with blank pages; when she tried to read it, she forgot how to read for a day.
- A king whose portrait was painted, but the artist could never capture his face — it always came out wrong.

# Resonance Silk Integration (Hypothetical)
No Sericari can weave Nihil silk. Attempts result in silk that unravels into nothing, often taking the weaver’s memories with it. Any Sericari who tries becomes a permanent Null.

# The Great Balance
Nullist extremists believe Nihil is the only truth — that all things must eventually return to the Final Silence. Harmonists hold that without Prima, Nihil would have cancelled reality long ago. The two are locked in an eternal, necessary tension.
