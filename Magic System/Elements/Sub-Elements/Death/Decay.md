# Description
Decay is the slow, creeping end. It degrades, rusts, and erodes. Often used for siege warfare and disposal.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You have advantage on checks to break objects.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can touch a non‑magical item and halve its durability.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You sense the age of any material.
