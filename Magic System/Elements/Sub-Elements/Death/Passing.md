# Description
Passing is the compassionate face of Death. It ensures a peaceful transition and protects against unnatural undeath.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You are immune to possession.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you may touch a corpse and prevent it from being raised as undead permanently.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can see the last few moments of a dead creature’s life by touching them.
