# Description
Brilliance is Light in its most absolute form. It denies shadow, lies, and half‑truths. Bearers of this variant are often uncompromising.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Unnatural darkness (magical) is suppressed within 10 ft.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you may attempt to dispel any illusion or enchantment by touch.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can sense the emotional “truth” of a spoken statement (not thoughts, but sincerity).

# Example Glyphs
| Glyph Name | Outlines | Effect |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Sun Lance | Amplification, Precision | A beam of concentrated light that deals radiant damage and sears magical darkness. |
| Truth‑Circle | Containment, Duration | Creates a zone where only truthful statements can be made. |

# Cultural Role
Brilliance users are often made judges or confessors. Some cultures fear them as living lie‑detectors.
