# Description
Blaze is fire’s fury condensed into a heartbeat. It is explosive, spectacular, and devastatingly brief. Where Flame is the steady candle, Blaze is the powder keg. Its mages are shock troops, demolitions experts, and living artillery.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your flames roar louder; you can cause your voice to carry over the din of combat as if amplified by a furnace’s roar.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can overcharge a fire spell to explode in a 10‑ft radius, dealing an extra 2d8 fire damage to all creatures in the area, but the spell’s own control ends immediately.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know exactly how much kinetic force a given explosion will generate.
