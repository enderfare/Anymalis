# Description
Density governs mass. Users can make themselves immovable or crush enemies with increased weight.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You have advantage against being pushed or knocked prone.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can increase your own weight tenfold for 1 minute.
