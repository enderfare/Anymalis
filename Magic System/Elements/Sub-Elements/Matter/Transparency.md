# Description
Transparency controls opacity and light transmission. It can render objects invisible, create lenses, or make armour see‑through. Essential for optical glyphs and espionage.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You can make your own clothing or a held item transparent at will for as long as you concentrate.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can turn a creature invisible for 1 minute, as per the Invisibility spell.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can detect invisible creatures or objects by a faint shimmer within 30 ft.
