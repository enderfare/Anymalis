# Description
State governs phase transitions. A State mage can melt steel, vaporize water, or temporarily turn a wall into smoke. It is the most physically dangerous Matter variant.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may change a 1‑foot cube of non‑magical material between solid and liquid (or gas) once per hour, provided it’s a pure substance.
- **Active:** Once per long rest, you can transform yourself into a cloud of vapour for 10 minutes, as the Gaseous Form spell.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can predict if a container will rupture from pressure or temperature.
