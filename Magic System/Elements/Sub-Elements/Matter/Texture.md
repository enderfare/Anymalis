# Description
Texture governs the feel and friction of surfaces. It can make walls slick, armor barbed, or counterfeit coins indistinguishable from real. Spies and artisans prize this variant.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** You may alter the texture of any non‑magical object you hold (silky, sticky, spiked) for 1 hour.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can make a 10‑ft square area either frictionless or extremely adhesive (difficult terrain or climbing advantage).
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You know the exact grit, polish, and wear of any tool or weapon.
