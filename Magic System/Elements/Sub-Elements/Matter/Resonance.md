# Description
Resonance, as a Matter variant, focuses on vibration and harmonic energy within physical substances. It can shatter glass with a note, reinforce structures, or store kinetic energy.

# Affinity Effects
- **Passive:** Your unarmed strikes deal an extra 1d4 thunder damage and can be heard up to 300 ft away if you choose.
- **Active:** Once per short rest, you can shatter all non‑magical glass and crystal within a 30‑ft cone.
- **Intuitive Understanding:** You can perfectly mimic any sound you have heard.
