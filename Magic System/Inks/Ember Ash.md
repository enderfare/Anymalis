# Description
Ember Ash is ink born from destruction. It is thick, warm, and smells of smoke. Mages who use it accept that every spell they cast might burn a little hotter than intended — sometimes the caster along with it. It is favoured by combat Scribes and Flamewoven Sericari.

# Effects on Glyphs
- **Amplification:** All damage dice are increased by one size (d6 → d8) when using an Amplification outline.
- **Backlash:** On a critical failure when casting, the ink ignites, dealing 1d6 fire damage to the caster and destroying the surface.
- **Elemental Affinity:** Fire glyphs cast with this ink deal an additional +2 damage per damage die.

# Common Uses
- Fireball variants, explosive runes, and siege glyphs.
- Wildfire and Blaze spells that are meant to spread.
- Intentional glyph‑shattering (Fracture outline combos).

# Resonance Silk Interaction
When Embercoil Sericari infuse their silk with Ember Ash, the silk gains the **Ignition Weave** trait: it can be set alight at will and burns without being consumed. The silk deals an extra 1d4 fire damage while ignited.
