# Drifters

*The People of the Road*

Drifters are defined less by blood than by movement.

They travel between settlements, regions, cultures, and Resonance zones, carrying stories, goods, techniques, and traditions with them. A Drifter may belong to a caravan, a travelling family, a merchant road, a wandering community, or simply a loose network of people who refuse to remain in one place.

## Adaptable

Drifters are accustomed to unfamiliar environments and rapidly changing circumstances.

When entering a new community or region, a Drifter can quickly learn local customs, practical techniques, and survival habits.

**Mechanical Effect:** Drifters gain proficiency in one additional practical or social skill of their choice.

## Roadwise

Years spent travelling give Drifters an instinct for routes, settlements, weather, danger, and the subtle signs that distinguish a safe road from a dangerous one.

**Mechanical Effect:** Drifters gain an advantage on checks made to navigate unfamiliar territory, identify common travel hazards, or determine a practical route.

## Improvisation

Drifters rarely have exactly what they need.

Instead, they learn to make use of whatever is available.

**Mechanical Effect:** Once per rest, a Drifter may improvise a simple tool, mundane object, or temporary solution from available materials, subject to GM approval.

## Resonance

Unlike many established lineages, Drifters possess no universal Resonance Affinity.

Their strength comes from exposure.

A Drifter raised beside Fire Resonance may learn Fire techniques. Another raised among Water Resonance may develop an affinity for Water. Others may never develop an affinity at all.

This makes Drifters particularly varied in their approach to Glyphic Resonance.

## Paths

Drifters can follow any of the Four Paths.

Common choices include:

* **Scribes** — travelling glyphwrights, mapmakers, historians, and ritualists.
* **Channelers** — scouts, guards, hunters, and caravan protectors.
* **Artificers** — mechanics, salvagers, engineers, and travelling craftsmen.
* **Nulls** — rare wanderers who use their resistance to Resonance to survive dangerous regions.

## Character Hooks

A Drifter character might:

* Be searching for a permanent home.
* Carry an important message between distant settlements.
* Have learned Resonance techniques from several cultures.
* Be following an old road that no longer appears on modern maps.
* Have inherited a strange object from a travelling ancestor.
* Know stories about places that most people believe never existed.

> **"Home is not where the road ends. Home is who waits beside it."**
