# Lineages

Lineages represent inherited biological and cultural identity.

A normal Lineage grants:

```text
1 Skill Training
1 Lineage Trait
1 Lineage Technique
```

A strongly specialized Lineage may additionally grant:

```text
1 Lineage Specialization
```

## Starting Limit

Lineage features cannot normally raise a starting Skill above Skilled.

Lineages should reinforce existing Anymalis identity rather than rewrite the universal skill system.

## Biological Statistics

### Animal Trait Score (ATS)

d10 scale, 0–10.

Each tier may grant +2 to one physical statistic and -1 to one mental statistic, plus appropriate sensory or natural-weapon features.

### Hybrid Trait Score (HTS)

d10 scale, 0–10.

### Quimera Percentage

```text
Q% = [ATS × 5] + [HTS × 3] + [RES ÷ 10]
```

Represents the likelihood of unstable offspring.
