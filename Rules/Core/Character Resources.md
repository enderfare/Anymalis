# Character Resources

Anymalis characters interact with magic through several distinct resources.

These resources should not be treated as interchangeable.

---

## SAN — Sanity

SAN exists for every character.

It represents mental and psychological stability.

SAN can be affected by:

- Trauma
- Psychological pressure
- Certain magical effects
- Forbidden techniques
- Other rules that explicitly target mental stability

SAN is independent from Glyph Fatigue.

---

## Animen (ANI)

Animen is the personal magical resource used by characters capable of directly manipulating Elemental Resonance.

Animen can allow certain characters to construct or manifest spells without conventional conductive Ink.

It is not an Element.

It is not a Variant.

It is a personal resource.

### Example

```text
Fire → Flame
+
Precision
+
Animen
```

The spell is still Fire. Animen only provides the conduction method.

---

## Glyph Fatigue

Glyph Fatigue represents the physical and metaphysical strain produced by manipulating Elemental Resonance through a naturally magical body.

It primarily applies to characters born with a functional Resonant Scar.

Typical symptoms include:

- Tremors
- Blurred vision
- Glowing veins
- Loss of elemental control
- Resonance instability

Extreme Glyph Fatigue may contribute to Hollow formation.

Glyph Fatigue is not SAN.

---

## How the Resources Interact

A character may experience:

### SAN loss without Glyph Fatigue

```text
Traumatic event
→ SAN decreases
→ Glyph Fatigue unchanged
```

### Glyph Fatigue without SAN loss

```text
Excessive Elemental Resonance use
→ Glyph Fatigue increases
→ SAN unchanged
```

### Both

```text
Overwhelming magical event
→ SAN decreases
→ Glyph Fatigue increases
```

Resolve the two effects independently.
