# Renown

Renown measures how widely a character is known.

| Rank | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Unknown |
| 1 | Familiar |
| 2 | Recognized |
| 3 | Renowned |
| 4 | Celebrated |
| 5 | Legendary |

Renown is neutral.

A hero and a notorious criminal may both have Renown 5.

## Interaction with Reputation

```text
Renown = reach
Reputation = interpretation
```

High Renown spreads Reputation farther.

Low Renown means even a strong local reputation may remain geographically or socially limited.

A character can therefore have:

```text
Renown: 5
Public Reputation: -4
```

or:

```text
Renown: 2
Public Reputation: +3
```
