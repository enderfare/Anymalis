# The Resonance World

The world of **Glyphic Resonance** is shaped by invisible currents of magic called **Resonance**, flowing beneath reality like unseen tides. Civilisation does not simply use magic — it has evolved alongside it, and every intelligent species is, in a sense, a product of resonance.

---

## The Eleven Elements
Magic is organised into **Eleven Elements**, each with a distinct set of **Variants**. The fewer variants an element possesses, the more concentrated and powerful it is.

| Element | Variants | Tier | Core Role |
|---------|----------|------|-----------|
| [[Light]] | 2 | Primordial | Truth, revelation |
| [[Darkness]] | 2 | Primordial | Concealment, absence |
| [[Time]] | 3 | Primordial | Moments, eternity |
| [[Death]] | 3 | Primordial | Endings, transition |
| [[Life]] | 4 | Fundamental | Growth, vitality |
| [[Matter]] | 5 | Fundamental | Substance, form |
| [[Temperature]] | 6 | Physical | Thermal balance |
| [[Fire]] | 7 | Physical | Destruction, warmth |
| [[Water]] | 8 | Terrestrial | Fluidity, adaptation |
| [[Earth]] | 9 | Terrestrial | Stability, endurance |
| [[Wind]] | 10 | Terrestrial | Motion, freedom |

Above all elements stand the **Mythic Meta‑Glyphs** — **Prima** (Creation/Possibility) and **Nihil** (Annihilation/Cancellation) — forces that govern the possibility of existence itself.

---

## Resonance Lineages
The peoples of the world are not simply “races”; they are **Resonance Lineages** — populations shaped over millennia by elemental saturation.

- **[[Felinnari]]** — feline wardens who believe *resonance must be controlled*.
- **[[Corveki]]** — avian archivists who believe *knowledge shapes resonance*.
- **[[Drifters]]** — adaptable humans who believe *resonance must adapt*.
- **[[Sericari]]** — silk‑weaving artisans who believe *resonance must be cultivated*; they are further divided into **Silk‑Lineages** attuned to each element.
- **[[Luminaris]]** — not a lineage but a condition, living repositories of Luminal Overflow.

---

## The Four Resonance Paths
Power is not limited to spellcasters. Four equally viable paths exist:

1. **Scribes** — traditional glyph casters, wielding Animent (ANI) and Glyphic Ink (INK).
2. **Channelers** — body‑resonance warriors who harness Strain (STRN) for martial anti‑magic.
3. **Artificers** — resonance engineers using tools, cartridges, and prepared devices.
4. **Nulls** — anti‑resonance specialists who disrupt magic and are immune to many enchantments.

Magic is powerful, but it is **slow, material‑dependent, mentally exhausting, and vulnerable to interruption**. A legendary swordsman who can shatter glyphs with a punch stands equal to a master mage who can freeze rain in time.

---

## The Veil War
A forbidden historical conflict where factions attempted to artificially create **Meta‑Glyph bearers** through resonance experimentation. Most records of the war are incomplete or contradictory. The war’s scars remain in the form of **Dead Zones**, unstable **Overflow Regions**, and the deeply entrenched **Harmonist** doctrine that both Prima and Nihil must remain dormant.

---

## Resonance Ecology
Long‑term exposure to elemental resonance reshapes everything: wildlife, climates, minerals, vegetation, and even thought patterns. Entire civilisations have diverged philosophically based on their environment — mountain cultures view resonance differently than oceanic ones, and volcanic peoples perceive it through fire and destruction.

> *“Resonance reshapes all things exposed to it long enough.”* — Core tenet of Resonance Ecology
