# Illuminated

*Those Who See the Resonance*

The Illuminated are defined by what they perceive rather than where they were born.

They are people changed by intense exposure to **Light Resonance**.

Some are born with the condition.

Others become Illuminated after surviving Resonance accidents, ancient rituals, prolonged exposure to Light-saturated environments, or contact with powerful Light Glyphs.

No single explanation accounts for every Illuminated individual.

## Appearance

Illumination manifests differently from person to person.

Common signs include:

* Luminous markings.
* Reflective eyes.
* Pale or glowing hair.
* Light beneath the skin.
* Geometric Resonant Scars.
* Shadows that appear slightly delayed.
* A faint glow around existing scars.
* Irises containing geometric patterns.

Some Illuminated barely appear different from ordinary people.

Others are visibly radiant.

## The First Sight

Many Illuminated communities mark the moment a child first perceives Resonance.

This event is known as **The First Sight**.

The child is traditionally taken somewhere quiet and asked to describe what they see without being told what they are supposed to see.

The purpose is not to teach them.

It is to determine how they naturally interpret Resonance.

The answers become part of the individual's personal history.

## The Veiled Room

A traditional Illuminated practice involves placing an individual inside a completely dark room containing several hidden Resonance traces.

The participant must describe what they perceive.

The ritual is called **The Veiled Room**.

It teaches an important philosophical lesson:

**Seeing something does not mean understanding it.**

Illuminated culture therefore places considerable emphasis on interpretation.

## Philosophy

Illuminated philosophy revolves around perception.

Their scholars often distinguish between three concepts:

**Seeing** — perceiving something exists.

**Knowing** — understanding what it is.

**Understanding** — knowing what it means.

They believe confusing these three leads to dangerous mistakes.

This has made many Illuminated individuals cautious investigators.

## Relationship With Light

The Illuminated possess a strong connection to the Light Element.

However, they do not all express the same Variant.

Their relationship with Light can manifest through:

* Revelation
* Illumination
* Perception
* Purification
* Reflection
* Other Light expressions

The specific Variant is determined by the individual's Resonant Affinity.

## Relationship With Nulls

The relationship between Illuminated individuals and Nulls is complicated.

Illuminated people are accustomed to perceiving Resonance everywhere.

Nulls represent an absence.

To an Illuminated observer, a Null can therefore feel like a hole in reality.

Some find this unsettling.

Others find Nulls fascinating.

Illuminated scholars are among the groups most interested in understanding how Nulls interact with Resonance.

## Society

Illuminated communities often form around places where Light Resonance is unusually strong.

These include:

* Ancient ruins
* High mountains
* Open plains
* Resonance observatories
* Old temples
* Abandoned laboratories
* Light-saturated regions

Their settlements frequently contain towers, mirrors, polished stone, crystal, and other reflective surfaces.

## Culture

Illuminated culture commonly values:

* Truth
* Perception
* Curiosity
* Understanding
* Responsibility
* Observation
* Scholarship

However, they do not necessarily believe that all truths should immediately be revealed.

A truth without context can be just as dangerous as a lie.

## Common Roles

Illuminated individuals frequently become:

* Investigators
* Researchers
* Scholars
* Archivists
* Glyph inspectors
* Explorers
* Historians
* Resonance analysts
* Temple keepers

## Character Hooks

An Illuminated character might have discovered a hidden Resonance pattern that nobody else can see.

They may be searching for the event that caused their Illumination.

They might belong to an observatory that studies ancient Light Glyphs.

Or perhaps they have seen something that should not be visible.

> **"The eye tells you what is there. The mind decides what it means."**
