# Description
The Sericari are a people shaped by extreme elemental environments. Their bodies naturally produce **Resonance Silk**, a conductive fibre that acts as a living glyph medium. Unlike other mages, they do not draw glyphs — they **weave** them, creating spells of unparalleled stability and duration.

### How to Choose
- You gain the base Sericari traits (Resonance Silk, Woven Glyphs).
- Select the element and one variant from its list. The variant determines your silk’s exact appearance, passive effect, and the **Intuitive Understanding** you receive.
- Your silk’s ability is described in detail in the respective sub‑element file under the **Resonance Silk Integration** section. For example, choosing **Fire → Ember** grants the ability to create long‑burning ember‑silk that deals persistent fire damage (see [[Ember]]).

## Woven Glyph Mechanics
When using their own Resonance Silk as a surface:
- **+2 Duration**, **+2 Stability**.
- Activation is 1 step slower (Action → 1‑minute ritual, etc.).
- Silk can store one active glyph per point of your Presence modifier.
