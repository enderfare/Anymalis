# Description
Embercoil Sericari hatch within volcanic resonance zones, their silk igniting under stress but never truly consumed. They embody fire’s duality: warmth and destruction. The Flamewoven are passionate, creative, and dangerously unpredictable.

# Role & Culture
They build forge‑temples near magma flows. Their society values artistic expression and controlled fury; an Embercoil who cannot master their own flame is considered a child. They craft weapons, art, and ritual pyres.

# Lineage Trait – Ignition Weave
Your silk may be ignited at will (no action required). While ignited, it deals 1d4 fire damage to any creature touching it (except you). You can extinguish it as a bonus action. Once per short rest, you can cause a silk construct to explode in a 10‑ft burst, dealing 2d6 fire damage.

# Choosing a Variant
The variant dictates the silk’s colour, burn pattern, and unique application. A **Hearth** weaver creates blankets that calm fear; a **Wildfire** weaver births a Calamity Cocoon. Your Intuitive Understanding also shifts—from sensing fuel reserves (Flame) to sensing the emotional “temperature” of a room (Hearth).
