# Description
The Luminaris are not a species but a **condition** — a living repository of magic born when Resonance overflows into a developing soul. This can occur in areas of Luminal Overflow, near incomplete Sericari rituals, or as a spontaneous mutation in bloodlines saturated with Radiance. Their bodies crystallise spell patterns into visible **Luminal Marks**, making them walking libraries of light. Feared, revered, and often hunted, they are living proof that magic can overwhelm the vessel meant to contain it.

## Origin of the Condition
Luminaris arise through several means:
- **Luminal Conception:** A child conceived during a Resonance Storm or within a Radiance‑saturated region.
- **Ritual Backlash:** A failed Sericari cocoon preservation or silk stabilisation ritual.
- **Ancestral Echo:** Descendants of a previous Luminaris may spontaneously develop the condition in adolescence.
- **Meta‑Glyph Exposure:** Proximity to Prima‑linked phenomena can trigger the marks to form in an otherwise normal individual.

# Physical Traits
The defining feature is the **Living Luminal Marks** — crystallised resonance structures beneath the skin. These marks:
- Appear gradually as the individual grows, often starting as faint freckles and deepening into intricate, glowing patterns.
- React to all magic usage nearby, pulsing or shifting in response to resonance.
- Evolve into shapes resembling frequently used spells, sometimes forming actual miniature glyphs.
- Can be any colour depending on the dominant elemental affinity absorbed at birth (see below).

Unlike tattoos or external glyphs, the marks are part of the body’s resonance signature. They cannot be removed without killing the host.

# Luminal Affinity (Elemental Imprint)
A Luminaris does not have a fixed element like a normal mage. Instead, their marks absorb the resonance of the dominant element present during their **Awakening** — the moment the marks first activate. This determines the colour and theme of their stored spells.

| Affinity Element | Mark Colour | Typical Stored Spell Themes |
|------------------|-------------|-----------------------------|
| **Light** | Golden white | Revelation, purification, illusion breaking |
| **Darkness** | Luminous black (absorbs light) | Concealment, silence, memory erasure |
| **Fire** | Incandescent orange | Explosive damage, warmth, passion |
| **Water** | Deep blue‑green | Healing, fluid movement, mist |
| **Earth** | Amber or grey | Protection, weight, fortification |
| **Wind** | Silver‑white | Speed, teleportation, sonic effects |
| **Life** | Radiant green | Regeneration, growth, empathy |
| **Death** | Pale violet or bone‑white | Necrotic damage, spirit communication, decay |
| **Time** | Bronze or shifting metallic | Haste, slow, temporal loops |
| **Temperature** | Gradient red‑blue | Thermal blasts, insulation |
| **Matter** | Crystalline clear | Transmutation, density control |
| **Prima (rare)** | All colours at once, shifting | Unpredictable creation effects |
| **Nihil (rare)** | Absence of colour, like a hole | Cancellation, anti‑magic |

A Luminaris may change affinity if exposed to a more powerful resonance source later in life, but this is extremely dangerous and often causes Identity Drift.

# Mechanical Integration

## Luminal Imprint System
- **Spell Storage:** You can store a number of spells equal to your Proficiency Bonus in your bodily marks. These spells must be ones you have witnessed or studied (you need not be able to cast them normally). Storing a spell requires 1 hour of meditation per spell level.
- **Reduced ANI Cost:** When you activate a stored spell, its ANI cost is reduced by 2 (minimum 1).
- **Instant Activation:** Stored spells can be cast as an action (or their normal casting time, whichever is faster) without needing ink or a surface. You are the surface.

## Echo Activation
Whenever you cast any spell — stored or otherwise — all of your Luminal Marks glow intensely for 1 round. This imposes disadvantage on Stealth checks and makes you visible to any creature that can see magical auras. The glow can be suppressed with a successful Concentration check (DC 15) but only for 1 round.

## Resonance Mixing
Once per long rest, you can combine two stored spells that share a border type (e.g., both have Flow, or both have Amplification) into a hybrid spell. The effect is negotiated with the GM but should be a logical fusion (e.g., Fireball + Haste becomes a blazing speed burst that damages enemies and speeds allies). Mixing costs 2 additional ANI and causes 1 point of SAN damage.

## Luminal Overload
If you ever store more spells than your Proficiency Bonus + 2, or if you cast too many high‑level spells in quick succession, you risk an Overload. Roll a Sanity save (DC 10 + number of excess spells). On failure, the GM rolls on the Overload Table.

### Overload Table (d6)
1. **Involuntary Echo:** A random stored spell activates immediately, centred on you if harmful.
2. **Resonance Leak:** All magic within 30 ft becomes unstable; spells have a 50% chance to fail for 1 minute.
3. **Identity Bleed:** You temporarily adopt the personality traits associated with one of your stored spells (e.g., fiery aggression for a Fire spell).
4. **Mark Rupture:** One Luminal Mark cracks, releasing 2d8 radiant damage in a 10‑ft burst and losing that spell until it heals (1 week).
5. **Critical Bloom:** All marks activate simultaneously; you are blinded and incapacitated for 1d4 rounds, and then enter a state of uncontrolled spellcasting for 1 minute.
6. **Luminal Cascade:** You must immediately release all stored spells in a single, apocalyptic burst. The GM adjudicates the devastation. This permanently reduces your ATS by 1.

# Cultural Role
- **Among the Felinnari:** Luminaris are seen as dangerous disruptions to the natural order; they are often isolated or assigned to high‑security warding posts where their power can be contained.
- **Among the Corveki:** Treated as living archives — sometimes literally bound into library‑sanctuaries. They may be revered as oracles or chained as living batteries.
- **Among the Drifters:** The most accepting, Drifter caravans often adopt Luminaris, using their flexibility to help the community.
- **Among the Sericari:** The primary origin of many Luminaris births. Sericari treat them with a mix of guilt and responsibility, believing they are the result of silk rituals gone wrong.

# Resonance Philosophy
> *“The body does not contain magic. Magic contains the body.”*

The Luminaris are a structural warning: when light cannot be contained, it begins to write its own existence inside those who carry it.
