# Description
Humans are the great adapters. They lack a fixed resonance identity, allowing them to imitate, hybridize, and innovate. Other species view them with suspicion but also rely on their trade networks.

# Personality
Ambitious, curious, dangerously unpredictable.

# Combat
Drifters fill any role; their flexibility makes them excellent Channelers who blend multiple techniques.

# Society
No ancestral homeland—they live as caravans, fleets, or multicultural hubs. Their settlements often become neutral ground.

# Magic
Many lack a dominant element but can learn to reduce cross‑element penalties, making them superb generalists.
