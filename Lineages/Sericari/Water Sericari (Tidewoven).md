# Description
Tidewoven Sericari are born near aquatic resonance fields—coral atolls, deep‑sea vents, and floating sargassos. Their silk flows, freezes, and mists, adapting to any liquid form. They are navigators, deep‑divers, and masters of fluid dynamics.

# Role & Culture
Their cities drift on tides or float beneath the waves. They are often sailors, fishers, and healers. An Oceanwoven’s mood can be read in the movement of their silk.

# Lineage Trait – Fluid Weaving
Your silk can move through water without resistance. You may shape silk into simple water‑walking platforms (supporting up to your weight) once per long rest for 1 hour. You never suffer penalties from being wet, and you can communicate basic concepts with aquatic creatures.

# Choosing a Variant
Selecting **Ice** turns your silk into a weapon of frozen shards; **Mist** makes you a phantom in the fog. Your Intuitive Understanding ranges from sensing currents (Flow) to locating the nearest freshwater spring (Rain).
