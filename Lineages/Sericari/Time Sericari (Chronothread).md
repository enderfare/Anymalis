# Description
One of the rarest lineages, Chronothread Sericari produce silk that reacts to temporal flow. Their colonies are hidden in time‑locked sanctuaries or near ancient recursive glyphs. They are historians, prophets, and wardens of causality.

# Role & Culture
Hourwoven settlements exist slightly out of phase with the world. They are patient to the point of agelessness, and their speech often sounds like prophecy. They despise reckless time manipulation.

# Lineage Trait – Temporal Thread
Your silk always knows its own age. A glyph woven with Chronothread activates 1 round faster than normal (minimum 1 action). Once per short rest, you may take the Dash or Disengage action as a bonus action while holding your silk. Additionally, you are immune to the *slow* spell.

# Choosing a Variant
A **Flow** weaver can speed up their allies and themselves; a **Still** weaver can encase a dying friend in a frozen moment until help arrives. Your Intuitive Understanding grants perfect timekeeping, or the ability to sense temporal tampering.
