# Description
Stormthread Sericari live atop floating cliffs, sky‑temples, and the very edge of the atmosphere. Their silk is light, fast, and often electrically charged. They are couriers, aerial hunters, and the voice of the sky.

# Role & Culture
Their homes are suspended by silk‑sails and kites. They prize freedom of movement above all; an earthbound Stormthread is a tragedy.

# Lineage Trait – Aerial Weaving
Your silk can remain suspended in open air indefinitely. You may create a “sail” of silk that doubles overland travel speed when used with a wind‑powered vessel or glider. Once per day, you can ride a current of silk‑threads to fly at 30 ft speed for 1 minute. You never take fall damage if you have at least one arm’s length of silk free.

# Choosing a Variant
A **Jet** weaver becomes a living rocket; a **Whisper** weaver becomes a master of secrets and sonic attacks. Your Intuitive Understanding can be anything from sensing air pressure (Vacuum) to predicting lightning strikes (Stormwind).
