## Example Characters

### Seraphine Vale — “The Woman Between Seconds”

**Affinity:** Time — Still  
**Birthmark:** Around right eye, resembling a shattered clock circle  
**Occupation:** Resonance Archivist at the Grand Archive of Elarion  

**Preferred Ink:** Silverthread (moonwater, powdered sapphire, silver dust) for extremely stable Time glyphs.  
**Surfaces:** Cathedral glass, thin silver plates, treated parchment; avoids skin.  
**Specialty Borders:** Duration, Echo, Anchor, Precision—famous for nearly microscopic borders.  

**Signature Spell — Frozen Verse**  
- Element: Time — Still  
- Ink: Silverthread  
- Surface: Glass panels  
- Borders: Anchor, Duration, Precision, Echo  
- Effect: For seven seconds, everything inside the glyph slows nearly to a halt (sound distorts, rain freezes, flames stop). Only Seraphine moves normally. After the spell ends, all stored momentum returns at once.  

**Weaknesses:** Physically fragile, requires preparation, overuse causes memory loss, strong emotions destabilize borders.

### Kael Draven — “The Walking Calamity”

**Affinity:** Fire — Wildfire  
**Birthmark:** Across chest and left arm like branching cracks  
**Occupation:** Former military glyph-engineer turned mercenary  

**Preferred Ink:** Ember Ash (volcanic ash, salamander blood, charcoal from battlefield ruins) for dangerously amplified spells.  
**Surfaces:** His own skin, iron chains, weapon blades; frequently burns glyphs into his flesh mid-combat.  
**Specialty Borders:** Amplification, Fracture, Pulse, Flow—considered reckless by scholars.  

**Signature Spell — Ash Cathedral**  
- Element: Fire — Wildfire  
- Ink: Ember Ash  
- Surface: Ground or walls  
- Borders: Amplification, Fracture, Connection, Pulse  
- Effect: Slams hand onto a surface, drawing branching fracture lines that spread like burning roots. Pillars of flame erupt unpredictably, nearby glyphs destabilize, heat spreads through resonance currents. The spell grows stronger the longer combat continues; even Kael cannot fully control where flames emerge.  

**Weaknesses:** Severe glyph fatigue, Wildfire resonance damages his nervous system, cannot safely use delicate glyphwork, emotion directly affects spell stability. Some believe he is slowly becoming a Hollow—a living wildfire glyph wearing human skin.
